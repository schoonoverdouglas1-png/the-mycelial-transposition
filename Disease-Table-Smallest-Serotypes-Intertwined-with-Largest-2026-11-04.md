# Best Disease Table — Smallest Serotypes Intertwined with Largest (2026-11-04)

- Built from everything done to date, Nov 4, 2026: pneumococcal deep research (report.md), Serogroup Table pasted + Corrected Copy 1 + Corrected Copy 2, Medical Accuracy Check, Unified Vaccine & Carrier Architecture PDF audit, toxoid postulate (vaccine-reaction / toxoid parallel, fourfold toxoid framing, logic-first doctrine), and the Shiro wild card redefinition.
- Originals untouched. This is a new table, old-with-new.
- Organism throughout, Latin binomial: *Streptococcus pneumoniae* (formerly *Diplococcus pneumoniae*).
- Disease terms are VACCINE-LEVEL, not per-serotype, per the deep research GAP finding: no retrieved label assigns a different Latin disease name per serotype. Do NOT read this table as a per-serotype Latin disease table. Where a vaccine has a hard restriction, it is stated in the row.
- Hard restrictions kept in every relevant row: PCV13 otitis media only for the seven PCV7 serotypes (1, 3, 5, 6A, 7F, 19A are NOT covered for otitis media by that label claim — wait, correction: PCV13 otitis media claim is for the seven PCV7 serotypes only; serotypes 1, 3, 5, 6A, 7F, 19A have no otitis media efficacy data in that label); PCV21 pneumonia excludes 15B, while PCV21 invasive disease includes 15B and 15C.
- Carrier note: CRM197 is a non-toxic diphtheria-toxin mutant. TT = tetanus toxoid, DT = diphtheria toxoid, Protein D = non-typeable *Haemophilus influenzae* protein D. A carrier is attached to the polysaccharide, not intrinsic to the serotype — see Break 1 audit.
- Fungal relationship: GAP for every row for a per-serotype fungal mapping. *Cryptococcus* serotypes A/B/C/D are a separate GXM system; no pneumococcal serogroup maps onto them in retrieved sources. Only the unnamed shared-determinant note exists.
- Bacterial Mitis relationship: DOCUMENTED only for serotype 5 in detail; PARTIAL for 12F, 15A, 18C, 33F (homology) and for 2, 4, 9A, 13, 23A (second paper, not opened in full); GAP for all other rows.

## Ordering method — smallest first, intertwined with largest second
- Disease serotype union from the deep research, sorted ascending: 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6A, 6B, 7F, 8, 9N, 9V, 10A, 11A, 12F, 14, 15A, 15B, 15C, 16F, 17F, 18C, 19A, 19F, 20/20A, 22F, 23A, 23B, 23F, 24F, 31, 33F, 35B (32).
- Intertwining: take the smallest remaining, then the largest remaining, repeat: 1, 35B, 2, 33F, 3, 31, 4, 24F, 5, 23F, 6A, 23B, 6B, 23A, 7F, 22F, 8, 20/20A, 9N, 19F, 9V, 19A, 10A, 18C, 11A, 17F, 12F, 16F, 14, 15C, 15A, 15B.
- That is why the table reads small–large–small–large, not simply ascending or descending.
