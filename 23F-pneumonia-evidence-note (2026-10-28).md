## Documented evidence that 23F causes pneumococcal pneumonia [D]
1. Pneumococcal community-acquired pneumonia (CAP) study, 70 patients: serotype 23F was the causative serotype in 8 patients — 3 from blood, 5 from sputum. Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4248763/
2. Serotype-specific urinary antigen detection in CAP: 23F appeared in 21 single-serotype episodes (25 including multiple-serotype episodes) out of 261 UAD-diagnosed pneumococcal CAP episodes. Source: https://publications.ersnet.org/highwire_display/entity_view/node/505434/full
3. Non-bacteremic pneumococcal pneumonia distribution: 23F in 5 of 272 non-bacteremic pneumonia cases (1.8%), 0 of 192 bacteremic cases in that study — present in pneumonia, not a bacteremia-only serotype. Source: https://journals.plos.org/plosone/article?id=10.1371/journal.pone.0072743
