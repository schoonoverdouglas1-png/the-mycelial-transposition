# Forward x Reverse — The Complete Organism Index

**Filed:** 2026-10-18 - For *The Mycelial Transposition* (creative manuscript)
**Frame:** educational manuscript reference only. Never medical advice. No diagnosis, no application to any person.

## His chain model [H — his thesis; the frame of this paper]

Douglas's model: **viruses are the small pieces** with which the American names and the old Latin names are literally connected. **Fungi, bacteria, yeasts, molds and things of the like get stuck to the chain in between** — attached between the names and the viruses — for the full picture. His assessment: *"we almost have it."*

The index below is built on that chain: **names -> (fungi / bacteria / yeast / mold stuck in between) -> viruses as the small connecting pieces**. Where the record shows no virus for a name, the link is marked [GAP] — the chain is open there, not closed by invention.

**His thesis on the connections [H]:** connecting fungi and bacteria to every entry automatically connects meningitis and pneumonia to all those things.

## His method: reverse reverse intellectualization of vaccine technology [H]

His words, his method: **reverse-engineer the vaccines** - start from what each vaccine was made to treat, and work backward to the immunoglobulin and serotype connections. His thesis: **what the vaccines were made to treat is where the rest of this data lies.** The vaccine-preventable disease for each entry reveals its immunoglobulin (IgG / IgA / IgM) and serotype connections. Part 3 works the vaccines backward; every entry below carries its immunoglobulin and serotyping lines forward.
