## Lou Gehrig's disease symptomology [D, stated generally, never applied to any person]

Progressive motor neuron degeneration: muscle weakness, fasciculations/twitching, spasticity, muscle cramps, dysarthria (speech), dysphagia (swallowing), respiratory failure; cognition typically spared; cause unknown (NIH); diagnosis clinical/exclusionary — no single definitive lab test.
