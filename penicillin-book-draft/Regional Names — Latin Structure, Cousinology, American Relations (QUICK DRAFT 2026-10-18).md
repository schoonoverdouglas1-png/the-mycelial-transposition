## Samples — three regional names worked

**Valley fever / San Joaquin Valley fever** — American name says the place (the valley). Latin: *Coccidioides* (the berry-like ones) — says the shape, not the place. The Latin and American don't translate each other; they describe different things [D]. Cousins: desert rheumatism (same disease, another American name) [D].

**Rocky Mountain spotted fever** — American name says the place (the Rockies) + the showing (spotted). Latin: *Rickettsia rickettsii* — says the man (Howard Ricketts), not the place or the spots [D]. The Latin is an eponym; the American is place + symptom.

**La Crosse encephalitis** — American name says the place (La Crosse, Wisconsin). Latin: the virus is *La Crosse virus* — the Latin kept the place [D]. Cousin: Jamestown Canyon virus — same serogroup, place-named for Jamestown Canyon, Colorado [D].
