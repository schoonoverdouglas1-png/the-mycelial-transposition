# TB Vaccines — BCG Strains, Serology, Mechanism (2026-10-17)

Research note for the manuscript. [D] = documented.

## The correction that frames everything

There is no "top five" list of distinct commercial TB vaccines — there is essentially **one**: BCG (live attenuated *Mycobacterium bovis*), made by many manufacturers as different **substrains**. TB has no serotypes (its tree is MTBC lineages L1–L8); BCG covers the complex broadly through shared antigens, not serotype matching. [D]

## The five major licensed BCG substrain products (>90% of world use)

All derive from the original Calmette–Guérin strain (Pasteur Institute, 1924), attenuated 1908–1921 over 231 passages. [D]

1. **Danish 1331** (AJ Vaccines, ex-Statens Serum Institut) — largest global share (~36%); noted for consistency. [D]
2. **Tokyo 172-1** (Japan BCG Laboratory) — Japan's strain; heat-stable, freeze-dried; WHO International Reference Reagent since 1965; used in 50+ countries. [D]
3. **Pasteur 1173P2** (French/Pasteur lineage) — high-potency strain used where TB pressure is greatest. [D]
4. **Russian BCG-I** (Microgen/Serum Institute of India lineage) — Russia, Europe, India; mother strain of the Indian and Bulgarian formulations. [D]
5. **Moreau RDJ** (Brazilian; e.g., Biomed-Lublin's Onko BCG) — Latin America; also used for bladder-cancer immunotherapy. [D]

Strains are used interchangeably with **no conclusive evidence discriminating efficacy or safety between them**; choice is historical/logistical. [D] But formulations differ measurably: a side-by-side study found Indian/Bulgarian vials (Russian mother strain) had >1,000-fold lower growth and fewer live bacteria than others, with different cytokine responses (IL-1β, IFN-γ). [D]

## Serology — the antibody story is not the protection story

- BCG's protection is **cell-mediated, not antibody-mediated**: Th1 immunity, CD4+/CD8+ T cells, IFN-γ supercharging macrophages. [D]
- BCG does stimulate some antigen-specific antibodies, but antibodies are not the correlate of protection. [D]
- **Serological (antibody) tests for TB are discouraged**: WHO (2011) recommended against commercial serodiagnostic tests — inaccurate. The working immune test, IGRA, measures **IFN-γ released by T cells**, not antibodies. [D]
- Book angle: the protection lives in the T-cell/macrophage axis — chasing antibodies for TB misses where the immunity actually is (rhymes with his section-55 doctrine: the chase stops at the wrong marker).

## How it works in the human body

1. **Intradermal injection** (not intramuscular) — the skin is an immune surveillance outpost rich in Langerhans cells and dendritic cells. [D]
2. **Antigen-presenting cells** capture the live bacilli, mature, and carry antigens to the lymph nodes. [D]
3. **CD4+ T cells differentiate into Th1** (driven by IL-12), with CD8+ cross-presentation; Th1 cells release **IFN-γ**, the key cytokine. [D]
4. **IFN-γ supercharges macrophages** to kill mycobacteria; containment ends in **granuloma** formation — the walled-off nodule (his "inclusions" have a cousin here). [D]
5. **Trained immunity**: BCG epigenetically reprograms innate cells (monocytes/macrophages), giving months of heightened responsiveness — the "off-target" protection against unrelated infections. [D]

## Efficacy, honestly

- Strong against severe childhood TB (meningitis, miliary — ~70–80% protection). [D]
- **Highly variable against adult pulmonary TB** (0–80% across trials/geographies) — latitude, environmental mycobacteria exposure, strain, and age all proposed. [D]
- WHO: single dose in infancy in high-burden countries; never in the US routine schedule. [D]
- Pipeline (NOT commercial): M72/AS01E (Phase 3), MTBVAC, VPM1002, H56:IC31 — none licensed for TB prevention as of Oct 2026. [D]
