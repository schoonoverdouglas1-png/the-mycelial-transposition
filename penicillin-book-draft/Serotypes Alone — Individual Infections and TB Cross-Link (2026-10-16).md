# Serotypes Alone — Individual Infections, Cross-Linked to TB Lineages (2026-10-16)

*Built at Douglas's direction for The Mycelial Transposition. His premise: read each serotype as its own infection entity — not always via pneumonia — because the tests measure them individually. Manuscript reference, never medical advice.*

**His premise (filed as his):** the serotype tests exist individually, so each serotype's infections should be read on their own — meningitis, otitis media, bacteremia, carriage — without pneumonia as the mandatory first association. Then that information co-mingles with the known *S. pneumoniae* serotype data, and cross-links to the TB lineages.

## Part B — the TB lineages (from the serotype-trees file, Oct 16)

TB has no serotypes; its tree is the *M. tuberculosis* complex branching into genome-defined lineages:

## Part C — the cross-link: what exists, what doesn't, his to build

**Documented (species level):** *S. pneumoniae*–*M. tuberculosis* co-infection is real, both directions — pneumo pneumonia can precede/reactivate TB; TB-damaged lung invites pneumo superinfection; cohort data places *S. pneumoniae* among the most common bacterial co-pathogens in TB patients. Filed Oct 16.

**NOT documented:** no literature maps *specific pneumococcal serotypes* to *specific TB lineages*. The cross-link at that resolution does not exist in print — which is exactly where Douglas's analysis goes, labeled as his.

## Part D — named diseases as serotype groups (his framing, Oct 16)

His point: **more than one serotype as a group can be part of the name of a thing like meningitis** — the named disease is the name of the *group*, and the serotypes are its members. "Meningitis" contains multitudes. Same with tuberculosis connected to the pneumococcal serotypes.

| Named disease | The serotype group inside the name |
|---|---|
| Meningitis (pneumococcal) | 3, 4, 14, 18C — plus 10A, 12F, 15B in the replacement era |
| Otitis media | 3, 6B, 9V, 14, 19A, 19F, 23F, 11A |
| Carriage specialists | 6B, 19F, 23F |
| Highly invasive | 1, 4, 5, 7F, 14, 18C |
| Penicillin-resistant / MDR | 6B, 9V, 14, 19A, 19F, 23F (+ 11A, 33F) |
| Replacement / surge | 8, 9N, 10A, 11A, 12F, 15B, 19A, 22F, 33F |
| TB-connected | all 23 — via documented species-level pneumo–TB co-infection |

Filed as his framing. The namology holds: one name, many members — and the members are numbered individuals with their own test results.
