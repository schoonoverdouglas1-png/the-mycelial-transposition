## Part 1 — His virus theory so far (distilled from his dictation)

1. **Viruses are both friend and enemy** — misunderstood; part of the healing process and of the healing-and-inflammation without which tissues cannot regenerate.
2. **Viruses are markers/indicators of infection** — they grow when we're sick and shrink when we get better; dormant in wellness, latent and *felt* in sickness.
3. **Viruses must be linked to fungal and bacterial things** — viruses *show* when those things are bothering living things.
4. **Duality of infection** — testing for one virus and continuing to find a second; the panel connects tests to find the two from the same vial of blood.
5. **The embedded immune system** — viruses as part of it, not invaders of it.
6. **The full cycle** — viruses must be activated for the immune reaction; the universal bugs get sick with our sicknesses, get better when we do, and on excretion become the cleansing, carrying toxins the blood alone cannot.
7. **The program** — complete concurrent testing → co-occurrence map: every virus placed alongside the bacteria and fungi it hangs out with.
8. **The central knot (hypothesis)** — the California serogroup viruses systemically linked to ALS and Lyme/Lou Gehrig's.
