# Serotype Trees — Streptococcus pneumoniae and the Tuberculosis Complex (2026-10-16)

*Compiled at Douglas's direction. Naming reference for the book — never medical advice.*

## The honest frame first

These two organisms are **not** classified the same way, and the book should say so:

- **Streptococcus pneumoniae** has **100+ serotypes** (Danish naming system) — each defined by its capsular polysaccharide coat. The "trees" that exist are **vaccine-coverage maps**: nested sets of serotypes, not a family tree.
- **Tuberculosis has no serotypes.** Its tree is the *Mycobacterium tuberculosis* complex (species) branching into **lineages L1–L8** (genome-defined families).

## Tree 1 — Streptococcus pneumoniae serotypes (the vaccine sets)

Serotypes are numbered in Danish-system discovery order — the numbers are labels, not biology. The sets nest:

| Set | Serotypes |
|---|---|
| **PCV7** (2000) | 4, 6B, 9V, 14, 18C, 19F, 23F |
| **PCV10** adds | 1, 5, 7F |
| **PCV13** adds | 3, 6A, 19A |
| **PCV15** adds | 22F, 33F |
| **PCV20** adds | 8, 10A, 11A, 12F, 15B |
| **PPSV23** (the 23-valent polysaccharide set) | PCV20's set **minus 6A**, **plus 2, 9N, 17F, 20** → 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6B, 7F, 8, 9N, 9V, 10A, 11A, 12F, 14, 15B, 17F, 18C, 19A, 19F, 20, 22F, 23F, 33F |
| **PCV21** (adult-focused, newest) | drops ten childhood-vaccine types; adds 15A, 15C, 16F, 23A, 23B, 24F, 31, 35B |

**Direct tie to his panel:** the Mayo test he ran (PN23M — *S. pneumoniae* IgG Antibodies, 23 Serotypes) measures exactly the **PPSV23 set** — the 23 serotypes above. His serotype comparisons were comparisons inside this tree.

### Notes on notable serotypes

- **3** — unusually thick capsule; the serotype most associated with vaccine breakthrough; still caused most invasive cases in older Danish adults after childhood PCV13.
- **19A** — rose to prominence *after* PCV7 arrived (serotype replacement: clearing some serotypes opens room for others); added in PCV13.
- **8** — surged in adults after childhood PCVs (not in PCV13); covered by PPSV23/PCV20.
- **6A / 6B** — a capsule pair; PPSV23 carries 6B but not 6A (partial cross-cover).
- **22F / 33F** — the PCV15 additions; 33F later notable in its own right.

Beyond the vaccines: 60+ further serotypes (15A, 23A/B, 35B, etc.) circulate outside every current vaccine — the tree is bigger than the medicine.

## Tree 2 — the tuberculosis complex (species → lineages)

No serotypes. The MTBC tree:

**Species of the complex:** *M. tuberculosis* (sensu stricto) · *M. bovis* (cattle; + the BCG vaccine strain) · *M. africanum* · *M. microti* · *M. caprae* · *M. pinnipedii* · *M. orygis* · *M. mungi* · *M. canettii* (the "smooth" TB bacilli).

**Lineages of *M. tuberculosis* (genome-defined):**

| Lineage | Name | Note |
|---|---|---|
| L1 | Indo-Oceanic (EAI) | "ancestral" clade; prominent on the Indian subcontinent |
| L2 | East Asian / Beijing | "modern" clade; a major driver of drug-resistant TB spread in Eurasia |
| L3 | East-African-Indian / Delhi-CAS | "modern" clade; eastern Africa, South Asia |
| L4 | Euro-American | "modern" clade; the most global — includes Haarlem, LAM, X-type, Ural families |
| L5 | West African 1 | *M. africanum* |
| L6 | West African 2 | *M. africanum* |
| L7 | Ethiopian | geographically restricted |
| L8 | animal-adapted lineage | the branch that stayed with animals |

## Namology notes for the book

1. **Pneumo's "tree" is a coverage map, not a family tree.** The serotype numbers record *discovery order under a Danish microscope*, and the groupings record *which ones the vaccines caught*. The names tell the history of the medicine, not the biology of the bug — exactly the kind of name-story his doctrine interrogates.
2. **TB's tree is geography wearing a genome.** Beijing, Delhi, Haarlem, Euro-American — the lineages are named for *places and peoples*, the convention his book already rejects. The L-numbers (L1–L8) are the neutral alternative sitting right beside them.
3. **Serotype replacement is the living argument.** When PCV7 cleared seven serotypes, 19A and 8 advanced into the empty room — the tree *re-grew around the medicine*. His imbalance-over-time thesis has a named, dated mechanism here.
