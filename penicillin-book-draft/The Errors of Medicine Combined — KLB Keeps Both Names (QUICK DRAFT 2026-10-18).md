## The KLB case — both names kept [H — your thesis]

**KLB needs to keep both names.** Because "bacillus" helps a person know it as part of that family — the rod-shaped bacillus family — and "bacterium," and our bacteria. Each name carries different information:

- **KLB / Klebs-Löffler Bacillus** — the working name: says what it looks like (bacillus = rod), says whose hands found it (Klebs, Löffler), says the family at a glance
- ***Corynebacterium diphtheriae*** — the taxonomic name: says where it sits in the order of things

Drop either one and information dies. Keep both and the person gets the family AND the taxonomy. That's additive naming as the fix for the errors, worked on the case you were looking at most.

The errors of medicine, combined, are one error: **renaming by replacement.** The fix is one fix: **renaming by addition.** Old name kept, new name added, both stand — for KLB, for the plague bacillus, for all of them.
