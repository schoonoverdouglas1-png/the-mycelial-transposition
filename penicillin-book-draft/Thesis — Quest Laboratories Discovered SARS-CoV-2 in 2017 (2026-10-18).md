# Thesis — Quest Laboratories Discovered SARS-CoV-2 in 2017 (2026-10-18)

**Status:** tested against the record. Filed as his thesis with the verdict.
**Frame:** *The Mycelial Transposition* — educational manuscript reference only. Never medical advice.

## His thesis [H]

Quest Laboratories discovered the SARS-CoV-2 virus in 2017.

## His supporting statement — TRUE [D, agreed → theorem]

The original SARS virus (SARS-CoV, the 2002–2003 outbreak) was known before 2010. Documented; his statement stands as stated.

## The three-way test

1. **Discovery record [D]:** SARS-CoV-2 was identified from the Wuhan cluster — reported December 31, 2019; genomes sequenced December 26, 2019 (BGI), January 2, 2020 (WIV), January 3, 2020 (China CDC); announced January 9, 2020; named SARS-CoV-2 by ICTV February 11, 2020. No 2017 discovery in the record.
2. **Quest's documented role [D]:** Quest Diagnostics launched its COVID-19 test service March 9, 2020 and received FDA emergency use authorization for its SARS-CoV-2 rRT-PCR test March 17, 2020. Quest was a tester at national scale — not the discoverer of the virus.
3. **What actually happened in 2017 [D]:** Hu et al., PLoS Pathogens, November 30, 2017 — five-year surveillance of a Yunnan bat cave found 11 new SARS-related coronavirus strains carrying all the genetic building blocks of the original SARS-CoV. These were SARS-related bat coronaviruses, not SARS-CoV-2.

## Verdict

The thesis breaks against the record on all three legs: wrong virus identified in 2017 (bat SARSr-CoVs, not SARS-CoV-2), wrong role for Quest (tester, 2020, not discoverer), wrong year for SARS-CoV-2 (2019–2020). The 2017 bat-cave paper is the real event nearest his date — a possible source of the crossed wire, filed as my inference, his to confirm or reject.
