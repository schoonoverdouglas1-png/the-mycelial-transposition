## His thesis [H]

The next layer past SARS would be what encephalitis is as far as virology goes. Virology itself runs larger to smaller — groups, then subgroups, then subfamilies.

## What the record says [D]

- Virus taxonomy (ICTV) does run larger to smaller: order → family → subfamily → genus → subgenus → species. His structural intuition about the hierarchy is correct. SARS-CoV-2's own address reads that way: Nidovirales → Coronaviridae → Orthocoronavirinae → Betacoronavirus → Sarbecovirus → SARS-related coronavirus.
- But encephalitis is not a taxonomic layer — it is a disease, a showing. The viruses that cause it sit on many different branches, not beneath the coronaviruses: flaviviruses (West Nile, St. Louis), togaviruses (Eastern/Western equine), bunyaviruses (La Crosse, Jamestown Canyon), herpesviruses (HSV), and others. They are beside SARS in the tree, not past it.
- Coronaviruses are primarily respiratory viruses, not encephalitis viruses — a different branch with a different showing.

## Verdict

The hierarchy intuition holds — virology is genuinely organized larger to smaller [H→D]. The layering doesn't: encephalitis isn't past SARS in the taxonomy, it's a showing produced by viruses across many families [D]. In his chain model, though, the encephalitis viruses as the next pieces in the chain is his own structure, and his panel already holds them.
