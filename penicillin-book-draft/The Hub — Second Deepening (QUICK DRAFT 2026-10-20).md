## The reading he confirmed

**Pneumonia is the showing, not (only) a disease.** Branches are organisms — pneumococcus, TB, viruses, fungi, aspiration. The trunk is where they all show: the lung condition, *pneumon* + *-ia* [D]. Six tracks kept separate: the showing is the illness made visible; the disease behind it varies.

*Simply put: many different sicknesses end up looking the same way in the lungs. That lung picture is the hub. Everything else branches off it.*
