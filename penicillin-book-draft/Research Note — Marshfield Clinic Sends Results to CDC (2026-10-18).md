# Research Note — Marshfield Clinic Sends Results to CDC (2026-10-18)

His claim (dictated Oct 18, 2026, ~1:31 PM local): "Marshall Clinic sends the results to the CDC in the state of Wisconsin. They're all tasked to do so... it's because they're trying to find a cure for this stuff desperately."

Note on the name: he renders it "Marshall Clinic"; the documented entity is **Marshfield Clinic** (Marshfield Clinic Research Institute / Marshfield Clinic Health System), Wisconsin. His correction open; the record below is on Marshfield.

## The three-way test

**1. Does Marshfield have a CDC relationship? [D]**
Yes. Marshfield Clinic Research Institute is a site in the CDC-funded U.S. Flu VE (Vaccine Effectiveness) Network — Marshfield's own research site states the network "is funded by the CDC (Centers for Disease Control and Prevention) and includes the Marshfield Clinic Research Institute" (marshfieldresearch.org). MCRF was the first network site to receive CDC funding (2004–05) and was the sole provider of this information for four flu seasons.

**2. Does Marshfield send test results to CDC? [D]**
Yes. Patient nasal and throat swabs collected at Marshfield are tested, and the results feed the national vaccine-effectiveness estimates — published in CDC's Morbidity and Mortality Weekly Report and presented to CDC's Advisory Committee on Immunization Practices (ACIP) by Marshfield researchers. That is literally Marshfield sending results into CDC.

**3. Does the "in Wisconsin" reporting path exist for lab results generally? [partial]**
The documented chain above is influenza/viral surveillance research, not arbovirus panels. In general, notifiable-disease results flow clinical lab → state health department (Wisconsin DHS) → CDC — but that chain was not verified for arboviral panels in this check. Marked [GAP], not forced.

## Tier ledger

Practical upshot for the Oct 19 visit and his three-lab repeat-panel plan: for reportable conditions, the reporting is automatic — it follows the ordering lab through the state chain to CDC regardless of which lab runs the panel.
