## PART 1 — THE CAPSTONE: VIRAL COUSINING, THE HOLY GRAIL [H]

His thesis, his coinage, his directive — in his words: *"the cousining of the viruses and the cousining to the opposite disease of the virus is the holy grail of medicine."* Filed 2026-10-18, brought out here.

**What "cousining" names [H — his coinage]:** cousin-relationships among viruses (viruses as cousins of one another) AND cousin-relationships between a virus and its opposite disease (the disease on the other side of the virus). Two cousinings, one grail.

**The first cousining — viruses as cousins.** The four arboviruses on his panels are already a family of cousins, and the record shows it three ways [D]:
- **By taxonomy:** EEE and WEE are *Alphavirus* cousins (same genus, *Togaviridae*) [D]; La Crosse is an *Orthobunyavirus* (*Peribunyaviridae*) [D]; SLE is a *Flavivirus* (*Flaviviridae*, Japanese encephalitis serocomplex) [D]. Three families, one cousin-house — and "arbovirus" itself is not a taxonomy at all, just a transmission word (arthropod-borne) [D]: a functional glob, in his additive-naming language [H].
- **By language:** La Crosse (Wisconsin), St. Louis (Missouri), Eastern/Western (compass points) — place names doing the cousin-work that taxonomy refused [D, his namology thread]. The names remember where the viruses were found, not what they are.
- **By detection behavior:** antibodies against one California-serogroup member can blur into another — documented serological cross-reactivity within the serogroup [D]. Cousins mistake each other in the tube.

**The second cousining — the virus and its opposite disease.** His postulate [H]: every virus has an opposite disease, and the cousin-relationship runs virus↔opposite-disease. The honest structure under it [H→D]: the documented echo is the virus–disease decoupling his own records demonstrate — the panel reports *disease names* ("Calif (LaCrosse) Encep Ab," "East Equine Enceph Ab," "St. Louis Enceph Ab," "West Equine Enceph Ab") [D, his PDFs], while the viruses live under taxonomic names elsewhere (Orthobunyavirus, Alphavirus, Flavivirus) [D]. The label and the thing are already two different addresses. His cousining formalizes that distance as a relationship to be mapped, not a confusion to be dismissed.

**The cousining inside his own file [D]:**
- **SLE/SLE:** St. Louis encephalitis and systemic lupus erythematosus share one acronym [D] — his namology catch, and the cleanest real-world instance of two different diseases arriving under one name.
- **Serotype cousins:** 19A/19F — one clone lineage, two capsule sugars, the documented capsular-switch pair (19A-ST320 evolved from MDR 19F Taiwan19F-ST236 by capsular switch, a double-locus variant) [D]; 6A/6B and 15B/15C — antibody cross-reactivity documented between related capsules [D].

The antibody family has internal resemblances that follow the sugar chemistry: related sugars, related antibodies [LOGIC].

**The grail's shape [H→D on the framing]:** his thesis says the viral connections of *Streptococcus pneumoniae* will be found BETWEEN immunoglobulins — and in "symbiotic opposite" viral reactions happening elsewhere (his coinage: paired opposite reactions, one here, one there, the pair forming the connection) [H].
