**Filed:** 2026-10-18 · For *The Mycelial Transposition* (creative manuscript)

## The chain

Every row runs the same chain: **American name → disease → symptomology → bacteria → fungi / yeast / mold → immunoglobulins → serotyping → virus (the end)**. The American name is the largest; the virus is the smallest piece. All 53 entries from the long list, each cell expounded as far as the record allows. [GAP] where the record is empty — the chain stays open, never closed by invention.

His naming directive [H]: "Lou Gehrig's disease" first, Latin second, everywhere.
