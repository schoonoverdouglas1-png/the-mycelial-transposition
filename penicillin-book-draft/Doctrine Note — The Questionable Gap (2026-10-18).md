## His theses [H]

1. **The chronically ill don't ask:** a patient sick chronically their whole life doesn't ask for testing unless very sick and trying to figure out what's wrong. (His big note.)
2. **The doctors he met** would mirror the most amazing doctors the King of England and Donald Trump could ever find — literally. (His assessment.)
3. **Civil liability blocks diagnosis:** they have the knowledge, tools, and medicines, but it doesn't jive because they can't legally agree to diagnose people anymore — because of civil liability.
4. **Under-prescribing as leverage:** with his friends the nuns at Sacred Heart Hospital, he leaned toward under-prescribing as leverage — literally attempting to force the doctors to give better medicine, and cheap medicine.
5. **Cheap medicine doctrine:** the best cheap medicine is the cost-effective way; a doctor can tell the patient the cheap thing will work and will make them feel better.
6. **His priority order:** cost savings first, and the fix at the same time second.
7. **Cheap subset testing:** we have to find a cheap way to test a subset of the population — because not 100% of people get tested for 100% of things.
8. **The computer must theorize gaps:** the computer has to theorize that there are gaps in testing, because not everybody gets tested every time they see their doctor.
9. **Gaps in control and standard both:** there are gaps in the control and in the standard — both.
10. **The questionable gap:** medicine has to have a questionable gap — that is the diagnostic reality AND the patient's lived-experience reality. (His coinage and doctrine.)
