## Part 1 — The enumeration: every cephalitis, two connection columns

For each entity: (1) its connection to pneumoniae/pneumonia, tested against the *S. pneumoniae* 23-serotype record per his standing pneumonia rule — partial serotype↔entity relationships reported as partial, never discarded, never upgraded; (2) the viruses documented as connected to it.

### 1. Pneumococcal meningoencephalitis (*Streptococcus pneumoniae*)

- **Pneumoniae connection [D — intrinsic]:** this IS the organism. Pneumococcal meningitis frequently extends into brain parenchyma — "meningoencephalitis" is the documented entity, not a coinage. Mechanisms documented: otogenic spread (middle-ear focus → meninges → brain; a gerbil model used a **serotype 3** strain, with histologically confirmed brain involvement and lethal encephalitis in 29% at low inoculum), hematogenous spread via bacteremia, and direct neurotoxicity from pneumolysin plus cell-wall components driving excitatory amino acids, free radicals, and massive apoptosis. Fatality 19–37% in reported series. [D — partial serotype note: serotype 3 is the one documented in the otogenic model; no serotype-specific human meningoencephalitis ranking found — GAP on the other 22.]
- **Viruses connected:** none intrinsic to the entity [GAP]. The virus coupling here runs through influenza: flu→pneumococcus lethal synergy has three documented mechanisms (filed in the all-vaccines workup), making influenza the virus most connected to pneumococcal CNS disease by route, not by entity [D].

### 2. *Mycoplasma pneumoniae* encephalitis

- **Pneumoniae connection [D — intrinsic, and the strongest pneumonia↔brain bridge in the literature]:** the agent IS a pneumonia — the classic "atypical" / "walking" pneumonia. Encephalitis is its most common CNS complication; a ~2,000-case pediatric encephalitis study found *M. pneumoniae* the most common identifiable cause; 5–10% of acute childhood encephalitis in Europe and North America is attributed to it. Mechanisms: direct invasion (upper respiratory tract → brain) and post-infectious immune-mediated injury; early-onset (≤7 days) vs late-onset (≥8 days) forms. Up to 60% of affected children carry persistent neurological sequelae.
- **Viruses connected:** none intrinsic [GAP]. Note the diagnostic overlap: *M. pneumoniae* encephalitis and enteroviral encephalitis can present indistinguishably in children [D].

### 3. The arboviral four — his panel's analytes (La Crosse, EEE, SLE, WEE)

- **Pneumoniae connection [GAP — none documented]:** no literature found for LACV/EEE/SLE/WEE predisposing to pneumococcal superinfection or sharing a route with pneumonia. The documented meeting point is the **syndrome** — pneumococcal meningitis and arboviral encephalitis inflame the same clinical neighborhood (fever, headache, altered consciousness, meningeal signs; EEE's literature explicitly includes meningitis; LACV's spectrum runs aseptic meningitis → encephalitis) [D]. By language they share the *-itis*; by taxonomy they share nothing with pneumococcus (acellular obligate parasites vs. Gram-positive bacterium) [D]. The synergy *pattern* (flu×pneumo) is his to argue by analogy [H]; the specific claim is [GAP].
- **Viruses connected [D]:** each other. EEE and WEE share a genus (*Alphavirus*); SLE's cousins are West Nile and Japanese encephalitis (same serocomplex); documented serological cross-reactivity within the California serogroup (antibodies against one member blur into another). La Crosse is the leading cause of pediatric arboviral encephalitis in the US.

### 4. Herpesviral encephalitis (HSV-1, HSV-2, VZV)

- **Pneumoniae connection [GAP]:** no documented route or synergy between herpesviruses and pneumococcus in CNS disease.
- **Viruses connected [D]:** HSV-1 is the classic sporadic encephalitis — temporal-lobe predilection, the textbook entity. VZV causes encephalitis particularly in the immunocompromised. **The richest virus coupling in this file:** HSV-1 encephalitis is a documented trigger for **anti-NMDAR autoimmune encephalitis** — 1–4 weeks after HSVE (choreoathetosis in children, psychiatric symptoms in adults; cases out to 7–12 months reported). A virus causing an autoimmune brain disease weeks later is the forward/backtrack marker made literal [H→D — his marker thesis finds its cleanest documented instance here].

### 5. Enteroviral encephalitis

- **Pneumoniae connection [GAP].**
- **Viruses connected [D]:** enteroviruses (incl. EV-A71, EV-D68) — the leading cause of aseptic meningoencephalitis, especially pediatric. Poliovirus is the historical member of this family with CNS invasion as its signature.

### 6. Cryptococcal meningoencephalitis

- **Pneumoniae connection [H→D]:** the route is documented — pulmonary cryptococcosis (inhaled yeast) → bloodstream → meninges; the lung is the portal, the brain the destination [D]. This is the organism that bridges his pneumonia list and his meningitis list in one body. His element split places meningitis in the fungal element [H]; cryptococcal meningoencephalitis is the entity where that placement has its most documented company [H→D]. CrAg (cryptococcal antigen) detects the organism's shed capsule polysaccharide in serum/CSF at ~98–99% accuracy — the antigen-direction test [D].
- **Viruses connected:** none intrinsic [GAP].

### 7. Tuberculous meningitis / CNS tuberculosis

- **Pneumoniae connection [D — by route and by name]:** pulmonary TB seeds Rich foci in the brain during bacteremia; rupture → meningitis. The lung-to-brain route is the documented path. And the namology: "tuberculosis pneumoniae" is his coinage [H]; pulmonary TB is the disease's commonest showing [D].
- **Viruses connected:** none intrinsic [GAP].

### 8. SARS-CoV-2 encephalitis / encephalopathy

- **Pneumoniae connection [H→D]:** COVID-19 pneumonia is the respiratory showing; neurological complications occur in roughly half of hospitalized COVID-19 patients [D]; direct CSF detection of viral RNA is rare — most cases are cytokine-mediated encephalopathy rather than direct neuroinvasion [D]. The pneumonia→brain connection here runs through systemic inflammation, not through pneumococcus.
- **Viruses connected [D]:** SARS-CoV-2 itself — CSF PCR-positive encephalitis cases documented (including one with no respiratory symptoms at all). His §218 thesis — every encephalitis connected *literally* to SARS-CoV and SARS-CoV-2, worked out with Dr. Obaid on the Quest encephalitis panel [H] — has this foothold: the coronavirus-brain connection is documented in case literature [H→D]. Honest seams: SARS-CoV-2 is not classified as an arbovirus [D] (his §217 "arbo side" is his extension [H]); "connected literally" and "every" are his, his to evidence.

### 9. Influenza-associated encephalopathy / encephalitis

- **Pneumoniae connection [D]:** influenza is the virus with the richest documented pneumonia coupling — the flu→pneumococcus synergy (three mechanisms) is the reason post-flu bacterial pneumonia kills [D]. Influenza-associated encephalopathy (notably in children, notably in East Asian literature) completes a triangle: virus → lung → brain, with pneumococcus riding the middle edge.
- **Viruses connected [D]:** influenza A/B themselves.

### 10. Autoimmune encephalitides (anti-NMDAR and others)

- **Pneumoniae connection [GAP].**
- **Viruses connected [D]:** the post-HSV trigger (above) is the documented virus→autoimmune bridge. Anti-NMDAR encephalitis also associates with ovarian teratoma (younger women) [D] — a non-viral trigger, honestly noted.

### 11. Parasitic cephalitides (Toxoplasma encephalitis; primary amebic meningoencephalitis — *Naegleria fowleri*)

- **Pneumoniae connection [GAP].**
- **Viruses connected:** none [GAP]. Included for completeness of the enumeration; the file does not force connections where none are documented.

### 12. Listeria rhombencephalitis

- **Pneumoniae connection [GAP].**
- **Viruses connected:** none [GAP]. Bacterial, foodborne, brainstem-predilect — the bacterial cephalitis that is NOT pneumococcus, kept here as the honest control: not every bacterial brain infection routes through the lung.

## Part 2 — His theses, filed as [H] with honest lines

**(a) §224 — encephalitis ALWAYS carries the three elements [H]:** "Encephalitis ALWAYS has fungal, yeast, and bacterial elements, in a balance that may or may not be easily detectable because the human's own immune system is attempting to heal and fix the patient (the healing masks the balance)." Filed as his, extending his §190 equilibrium thesis into the brain. Honest lines: the literature classifies encephalitides by single etiology [D]; genuinely mixed CNS infections are documented in specific contexts (e.g., cryptococcal disease in advanced HIV, where fungal + viral + bacterial co-presence is the clinical reality) [D] — but the *universal* claim is his, his to evidence. "June" ("Many a June cephalitis") remains filed verbatim — genuine or the month of arboviral season — his correction still open [GAP].

**(b) §218 — the Quest/Obaid account [H]:** every encephalitis on the panel connected literally to SARS-CoV and SARS-CoV-2; encephalitis testing and virus testing are two different technical things; Quest PCR "with biology" used to work backwards through the viruses, trying to save his ex-wife from a spinal tap. Filed as his account. Honest foothold [H→D]: serology (antibody) vs. PCR (nucleic acid) ARE two different technical things — the distinction is real laboratory medicine [D]. The universal SARS connection is his [H].

**(c) VNA = viral nucleic acid [H]:** his definition, filed. Standard usage: "viral nucleic acid" is what PCR detects [D] — his expansion matches the term's content.

**(d) "Encephalitis is part of all viruses that make you sick" [H]:** filed verbatim ("101% connected and 100% connected"). Honest line: encephalitis/encephalopathy is a documented complication of many systemic viral infections — influenza, measles, SARS-CoV-2, enteroviruses [D] — but "all" overstates what the record holds; the universal is his, his to evidence.

**(e) §194 — the Quest lab-department account [H]:** the head of Quest's lab department told Dr. Obaid that all encephalitides have an underlying fungal element requiring azoles; the original testing allowed azole susceptibility runs per organism; Quest recommended no specific medicine. ## Part 3 — Synthesis

**Most connected to pneumoniae, ranked:**
1. *M. pneumoniae* encephalitis — the agent IS a pneumonia; the single strongest documented pneumonia↔brain bridge [D].
2. Pneumococcal meningoencephalitis — the organism IS pneumoniae; lung/blood/ear → brain by documented routes [D].
3. Cryptococcal meningoencephalitis — lung portal → bloodstream → meninges [D].
4. Tuberculous meningitis — pulmonary TB → Rich foci → meningitis [D].
5. Influenza encephalopathy — via the flu→pneumococcus synergy triangle [D].
6. SARS-CoV-2 encephalopathy — via COVID pneumonia and systemic inflammation [D].
7. The arboviral four, HSV/VZV, enteroviruses, autoimmune, parasitic, Listeria — [GAP] on pneumoniae connection; honestly unconnected in the record, not forced.

**Richest virus couplings:** the arboviral four with each other (his panel's universe) [D]; HSV-1 → anti-NMDAR autoimmune encephalitis, the documented virus-to-autoimmune bridge and the cleanest instance of his forward/backtrack marker thesis [H→D]; SARS-CoV-2's documented (if rare) direct neuroinvasion [D].

**Where the GAPs cluster:** (1) arbovirus↔pneumococcus synergy — the pattern exists for flu, not for LACV/EEE/SLE/WEE; (2) serotype-specific CNS rankings beyond serotype 3's animal-model appearance — 22 serotypes unmapped to brain disease; (3) the parasitic cephalitides, included but unconnected; (4) "June" — genuine or month, his correction open; (5) the universal claims (§224's "always," §218's "every," "all viruses") — filed as his, evidence his to bring.

**The honest ledger of this reassessment:** the pneumonia↔cephalitis bridge is real and load-bearing in exactly the places the literature puts it — atypical pneumonia, pneumococcus itself, the lung-portal fungi, TB's dissemination, flu's synergy. Where his theses extend beyond the record, they are tagged and his. Nothing was forced to connect.
