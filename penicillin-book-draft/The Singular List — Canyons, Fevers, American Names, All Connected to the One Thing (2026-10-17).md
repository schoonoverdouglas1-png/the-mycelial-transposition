### Row 3, deepened — Valley Fever as its own disease (his commission, Oct 17)

His words: *"Add valley fever into this because valley fever is a different disease and I want the symptomology and the fungi and bacteria and bacterium and pneumonia and pneumonia as a word, it was analyzed to AI, probably explain the rest is crap."* Filed: Valley Fever is a DIFFERENT disease — its own entity, its own row, not folded into any other. (His symptomatic-sameness thesis with Jamestown Canyon lives on the illness track; the disease entity stands apart — his five tracks holding both.)

**The symptomology [D]** (sourced lists — CDC/MSD/Wikipedia clinical summaries):
- 60% of infected: minimal to no symptoms. 40%: clinical illness, primarily respiratory — bronchitis/pneumonia-like, usually resolving in weeks.
- The notable: profound tiredness (fatigue persisting months), fever, cough, headache, rash, muscle pain, joint pain, loss of smell and taste, night sweats, weight loss, chest pain, shortness of breath.
- The classic triad — **"desert rheumatism"**: fever + joint pains + **erythema nodosum** (the red nodules) [D].
- 3–5%: chronic — fibrocavitary lung disease (productive cough, fevers, night sweats, weight loss); disseminated — skin, bone/osteomyelitis (spine), meninges (coccidioidal meningitis, months to years later).
- **"Nothing announces a fungal cause"** — symptoms overlap almost entirely with other pneumonias [D]. His tested-showing thesis, wearing a white coat: the fungus hides inside the pneumonia showing.

**The fungi [D]:** *Coccidioides immitis* and *C. posadasii* — dimorphic soil fungi of the southwestern US (Arizona, California's central valley, New Mexico, Texas west of El Paso, into northern Mexico; foci in Central/South America, Utah, Nevada, south-central Washington). Airborne **arthroconidia** (spores) inhaled after soil disturbance; not contagious person-to-person [D]. ~20,000 cases reported to CDC annually [D].

**The bacteria and bacterium [D + GAP]:** secondary **bacterial** superinfection of coccidioidal lung cavities is the documented bacterial chapter — the fungus cavitates, bacteria move into the cavity (the post-TB aspergillosis pattern, his triad tree's territory). The specific bacterium-to-fungus mapping — which bacteria, which cavities, in what order — is [GAP — his to fill]. His "bacteria and bacterium" phrasing kept: the bacterial world around the fungus, and the single bacterium to be named.

**Pneumonia [D]:** coccidioidomycosis causes an estimated **15–30% of community-acquired pneumonia** in highly endemic Arizona metro areas (Phoenix/Tucson); ~20% in endemic regions broadly. The Mayo Phoenix review: 24% of hospitalized CAP patients were never tested for it; 10% of those tested were positive — and early identification shortened hospital stays [D]. **No vaccine exists** [D].

**Pneumonia as a word — analyzed [D]:** Greek ***pneumon*** (lung, also breath/air) + ***-ia*** (condition) = **the lung condition**. That's the whole word: the organ + the condition. His judgment filed as his: *"the rest is crap"* — the verbiage around the word, the hedging and the Latinate fog, against the word itself, which says plainly what it is. The unhidden Latin (his 127): the lung, named.
