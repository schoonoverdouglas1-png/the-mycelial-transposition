# THE FULL TREE — Viruses to Microbes (2026-10-17)

*The capstone. Built at Douglas's commission (dictated-notes section 97): "that full tree is what I'm trying to build and this is what I'm trying to get at." Manuscript reference — creative theoretical work, never medical advice. No treatment or diagnostic content.*

## His commission (his words)

*"Make a list of viruses and their known companions, whether it's diseases by name and then if there are diseases by name, associated with those viruses, connect them to older names that are close that match or match exactly and then show the fungus, bacteria, pneumonia, or tuberculosis that's connected to each one of them."*

## How to read this tree

Five levels, every chain:

- **L1 — Virus:** the human virus, with its arboviral cousins noted (family + genome in parentheses).
- **L2 — Known companions:** co-pathogens / associated microbes documented with it.
- **L3 — Diseases by name:** the diseases associated with it, modern names.
- **L4 — Old names:** older names — close, matching, or exact — from the namology record.
- **L5 — Microbial anchors:** the fungus, bacterium, pneumonia (serotype where the map has one), or tuberculosis connected to the chain. Cross-linked backward to the source tree.

Tier tags: **[D]** documented · **[H]** his · **[H→D]** his with documented foothold · **[GAP — his to fill]** the record ends here. His 88% bar: nothing below it unless marked gap. Empty branches are shown empty — the tree displays its own incompleteness, per his method (partials reported, never upgraded).

## His theses framing the tree

- **(89)** Chase not just the pathogen but the pathogens it's *connected to* — the network, not the node.
- **(90)** The rehearsal principle: the body mimics disease to know it first, so it can fight it later.
- **(91)** Purposeful colonization: the body *keeps* bacteria, fungi, yeasts as preparedness; the colonists are part of pneumonia itself.
- **(92)** Test pneumonia against the Mayo/Quest 23-serotype panels; partial relationships must be examined, never discarded, never upgraded.
- **(94–95)** Immunity works through nucleic-acid relatedness between viral cousins; **VNA** (Viral Nucleic Acid) names the shared element.
