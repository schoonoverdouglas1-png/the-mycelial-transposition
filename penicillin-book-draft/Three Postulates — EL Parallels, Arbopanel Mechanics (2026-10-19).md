## Postulate 1 [H]: Encephalitis lethargica parallels Lou Gehrig's disease exactly

**Verdict:** doesn't hold in the record. Encephalitis lethargica's documented signature sequela was post-encephalitic parkinsonism — rigidity, oculomotor trouble, sleep disturbance — not motor neuron disease [D]. Different clinical picture, different pathology. What IS shared: both are neurodegenerative mysteries, and EL's cause was never found (the paper just delivered: a million cases, no virus). Filed as his parallel, untouched.

## Postulate 2 [H]: The arbopanel uses viruses to detect viruses

**Verdict:** close, inverted. The Mayo panel is an indirect immunofluorescence assay: slides carry viral antigens, patient serum goes on, and what gets detected is antibodies — the byproducts — binding the antigens [D]. So it uses viruses (antigens) to detect the body's response to viruses, not viruses directly. His framing filed as his; the precise mechanics beside it.

## Postulate 3 [H]: The arbopanel looks at the bugs to see if the bugs are sick — the bugs are sick with the same things we're sick with. The actual mechanics, if it wasn't understood.

**Verdict:** not what the human panel does — it tests human serum, not bugs [D]. But the mechanics he describes are real one step over: public-health mosquito surveillance genuinely tests bug pools for West Nile, La Crosse, EEE — the same viruses that sicken us [D]. The bugs ARE checked for sickness with the same things we're sick with; it just happens in surveillance programs, not in his blood panel. [H→D]

This also rejoins his 1945-virus thesis (bugs sick just like people) — filed separately, standing.
