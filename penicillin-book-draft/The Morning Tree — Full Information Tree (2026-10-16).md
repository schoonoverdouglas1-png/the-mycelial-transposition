## BRANCH 3 — THE SHOWING (symptomology doctrine)

### 3a. The doctrine [H] (55)
1. The chase doesn't end at the virus — it starts there. Doctors hunt until they find a virus and stop; that's the mistake ("satisfaction of search" **[D]**).
2. Cross-link backwards from the virus to the cause (reverse causal reasoning — Koch, Bradford Hill **[D]**).
3. Symptomology to the forefront — what doctors hear most and think about silently most.
4. *"Clinical diagnosis has to outrank laboratory testing in 101% of the time"* — his emphasis, filed verbatim. (**[D]** tradition: Osler; honest note: labs do sometimes overturn the clinical call — state it as his doctrine.)

### 3b. The symptom quartet (from this conversation)
| Disease | Showing | Naming tradition |
|---|---|---|
| Osteomyelitis | Fever, deep bone pain, swelling/redness/warmth over bone, drainage; bloodstream arrival (Staph aureus) | Greek anatomy: bone-marrow-inflammation |
| Rickettsial (RMSF) | Sudden fever, severe headache, muscle aches; bleeding-spot rash, wrists/ankles inward; vessel attack body-wide | Eponym: Howard Ricketts (breaks the Latin pattern) |
| Systemic lupus | Fatigue, fever, joint swelling, butterfly facial rash, flares/remissions | Latin animal: wolf (13th c., rash like a wolf's bite) |
| Scarlet fever | Strep throat + fever, then sandpaper rash in folds, strawberry tongue; toxin via phage T12 | Named for the showing itself (the scarlet rash) |

- **[H]** Four rashes, one category of showing — the clinical read of the rash outranks the lab (doctrine in action).
- **[H]** Shared showings (fever through all four; joint/muscle pain through three) = conjoined symptomology. Standing honest line: overlapping presentation, not shared cause.
- **[H→D]** Rickettsiae are obligate intracellular bacteria — the bridge organism between his bacterial and viral material. Scarlet fever's toxin gene rides a virus inside the bacterium — the symbiosis from the other direction.
- Factual flag: doxycycline — half of his age-14 "super medicine" — is the established first-line treatment for rickettsial disease (CDC). His thesis intersecting the record; not a recommendation.

### 3c. Scarlet fever × the serotypes (from this conversation)
- **[D]** Scarlet fever is *Streptococcus pyogenes* (Group A) — a different species from *S. pneumoniae*. No serotype-to-scarlet-fever map exists.
- **[H→D]** The real connection: same genus; cousin typing systems (pneumococcal serotypes ↔ pyogenes M/emm types); the rash comes from the toxin side (phage-carried speA), not the capsule side.
