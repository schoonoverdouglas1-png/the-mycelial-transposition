# Doctrine — Testing and Treatment Belong to Clinician and Patient

**Filed:** 2026-10-18 · For *The Mycelial Transposition* (doctrine)
**Source:** Douglas's dictation, main chat, ~11:43 AM CDT — his words, filed verbatim in substance:

## His doctrine [H]

"Testing and treatment options belong to the clinician and always the patient, because the patient has to agree to those things in order for them to be consensual. And for medicine to work, the patient has to be able to make decisions just like the doctor does. And they have to be part of the decision-making process."

## Standing with the record [D]

Shared decision-making and informed consent are established principles of medical ethics and clinical practice — his doctrine parallels them. Filed as his formulation; the parallel is documented.

## Placement

Belongs in the book's doctrine/front matter alongside the two-pass method and the cure doctrine — the consent thread already runs through the work (Nuremberg Code, informed consent in the Quantum Medicine dedication; "test/treatment decisions belong to clinicians" headers on every clinical workup). This statement completes it: clinician AND patient, consent as the mechanism, the patient as decision-maker.
