## Why resistance exists — the medical reasons (verified)

1. **Altered penicillin-binding proteins.** Penicillin kills by binding PBPs (1a, 2b, 2x and others), the enzymes that build the cell wall. Resistant pneumococci carry PBPs with **reduced affinity** — the drug can't grip its target. (PMC 2005; IJPSR review)
2. **Mosaic genes via transformation.** The altered *pbp* genes are mosaics — native pneumococcal DNA recombined with divergent blocks from closely related streptococci (*S. mitis*, *S. oralis*). *S. pneumoniae* is naturally transformable: it picks up foreign DNA and splices it into its own chromosome. Resistance is literally borrowed and stitched in. (PMC 2005; PNAS 2023)
3. **murM.** A functional *murM* gene (alternative cell-wall substrate) is required to express the resistance; mosaic *murM* can dramatically raise the level. (PMC 2005)
4. **Selection pressure.** Widespread beta-lactam use kills the susceptibles and leaves the altered — the resistant clones then spread globally as named international clones (Spain23F-1, Spain6B-2 and others). Overuse of penicillin is the main contributor. (microbewiki/Koebel et al.)
5. **Tolerance mutations.** Non-PBP mutations (e.g., iron-permease nonsense mutations reducing antibiotic-induced oxidant accumulation) can precede and enable the major PBP changes — the bug first learns to survive the drug's blast, then to ignore it. (ResearchGate/Fani et al.)

## Theorizing — in his framework (labeled as theorizing, not established)

- **Resistance is the bug learning to live with the medicine.** The drug arrives as an event; the susceptibles die; the altered survive and inherit the room. His "clearing one thing opens room" mechanism, with the medicine itself doing the clearing.
- **Borrowed armor.** The mosaic *pbp* genes are stitched together from neighboring streptococci — the bug doesn't invent resistance alone, it *collects* it from the bugs it lives among. His embedded-bugs thesis, at the genetic level: the community teaches the individual.
- **Rotation around the drug.** The resistant serotypes are the ones that rotated around penicillin instead of being stopped by it — his rotational precession, with the medicine as the fixed point and the disease moving around it.
- **Imbalance over time.** Every course of antibiotics is a pressure event; resistance is the imbalance made heritable. Old (the pre-antibiotic susceptible world) and new (the mosaic world) looked at together — exactly his rule.
- **Chain of custody.** Who decided the dosing, who finished the course, what ran off into the water — the custody chain of the drug itself is part of why the resistant serotypes exist.
