## PART 2 — ALS SYMPTOMOLOGY, THE DOCUMENTED PICTURE [D, stated generally, applied to no person]

Amyotrophic lateral sclerosis (ALS, Lou Gehrig's disease): a progressive neurodegenerative disease of upper and lower motor neurons [D]. The documented clinical picture:

- **Progressive muscle weakness**, beginning focally and spreading [D].
- **Fasciculations** — visible muscle twitching — and muscle atrophy [D].
- **Dysarthria** (speech difficulty) and **dysphagia** (swallowing difficulty) as bulbar motor neurons fail [D].
- **Respiratory failure** — the usual terminal event, as respiratory muscles weaken [D].
- **Cognition typically spared** — the disease attacks motor neurons, not thought [D].
- **Cause officially unknown** (NIH) [D].
- **Diagnosis is clinical and exclusionary** — EMG and MRI support, but no single definitive laboratory test establishes it [D]. His read, filed beside it: "lab testing not nailed down yet" [H].

This picture is stated here as the documented entity, on its own track (disease). Nothing in this file connects it to any person.
