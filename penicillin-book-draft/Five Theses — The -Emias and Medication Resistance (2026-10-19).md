## Thesis 1 [H]: "Medication resistance" — his coinage

**His thought:** chemical imbalance in the body creates the phenomenon he calls *medication resistance* — not the bug resisting the drug, but the body's chemistry resisting the medication.

**Combined:** resistance isn't always in the bug — sometimes it's in the chemistry around the drug. His coinage names the host-side half that antimicrobial-resistance doesn't cover.
