## Postulate 1 — The catalog story [H — his account]

He and Dr. Obaid were looking at different test manufacturer catalogs, and each chose differently-named tests containing the same things. He chose the **meningitis test** — brand new, from a year-old catalog. The year after that, it was called a goddamn **arbopanel**. This is the origin story of his "Encephalopathy Meningeal Testing" rename thesis: the test he picked as meningitis testing got renamed out from under him.

## Postulate 2 — The naming discussion [H — his account]

They had a lengthy discussion about where these viruses came from — because of **where they got names and why they got names**. His namology principle: a virus's name encodes its origin story, and the origin story is part of the evidence.

## Postulate 3 — The 1945 virus [H — his thesis]

When you're using a virus discovered in 1945 and it suddenly pops up and shows in a specific test, consider: **the bugs were sick just like people** — because they were most likely measuring human blood in the bugs, and the viruses that were in people would then be in those bugs. The vector's sickness mirrors the human's.

## Postulate 4 — Wood ticks are getting sick from us [H — his thesis]

You find a swirl of sick bugs around tested people on the outside — like wood ticks. **Wood ticks are getting sick from us.** The direction of sickness runs human → bug, not only bug → human. (His reversal of the standard vector narrative.)

## Postulate 5 — Disease onset secondary from a technical allergic reaction [H — his coinage and thesis]

We're sick with whatever they have; the bugs create an allergic reaction with their venoms, and then the bacteria start showing. That sequence — venom, allergic reaction, bacterial showing — is what he calls **"disease onset secondary from a technical allergic reaction."** It persists until we get rid of the toxin.

*Honest neighbor [D, stated generally]:* arthropod saliva does provoke immune responses and can facilitate pathogen establishment — the allergy-before-infection ordering has a documented echo. His venom→allergy→bacteria sequence as the mechanism of disease onset is his [H].
