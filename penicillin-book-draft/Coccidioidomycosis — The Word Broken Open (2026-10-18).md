## His thread [H]

"Idiom, of course, has to hang out with mycosis." Filed as his — the idiom hangs out with the -mycosis words. (My reading; his to correct.)

## The word broken open [D]

**Coccidioidomycosis** = *coccidio-* + *-oides* + *-mycosis*:

- *kokkis* (Greek) — berry, little grain
- *-oides* — like, resembling → *Coccidioides* = "the berry-like ones" (named for the round spherules)
- *-mycosis* — the fungal condition

The full reading: **the fungal condition of the berry-like ones.**

## The American name [D]

**Valley fever** — San Joaquin Valley fever; also desert rheumatism. The American name says the place; the Latin says the shape.

## The organism [D, stated generally]

*Coccidioides immitis* / *Coccidioides posadasii* — dimorphic fungi; the spherules carry endospores. Documented to reach the meninges (meningeal coccidioidomycosis).

## The -mycosis family it hangs out with [D]

Aspergillosis, candidiasis, cryptococcosis, histoplasmosis, blastomycosis, mucormycosis — the idiom family: each one names the fungus + the fungal condition, the same shape of word every time.
