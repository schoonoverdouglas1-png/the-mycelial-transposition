Etymology: *osteo-* (Greek *osteon*, bone) + *myelo-* (Greek *myelos*, marrow) + *-osis* (condition/process; the suffix of fungal disease names).
1. A disease condition of bone and marrow of fungal character; the fungal-element counterpart to *osteomyelitis*. [His coinage.]
