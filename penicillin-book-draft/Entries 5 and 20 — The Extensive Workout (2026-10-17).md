## Entry 5 — Meningococcal disease — *Neisseria meningitidis*

**The entry, repeated verbatim from the Master List:**

> Old names: cerebrospinal fever, spotted fever [D]. → Organism: *N. meningitidis*, the diplococcus [D]. → Fungi: [GAP]. → Bacteria: *N. gonorrhoeae* — same genus, different showing [D]. → Viruses: [GAP]. → Vaccines: Menomune (MPSV4, 1981) [D]; Menactra/Menveo conjugates (post-1997) [D]. → Serotypes: serogroups **A, B, C, Y, W-135** [D] (1997 vaccine: A/C/Y/W-135; B uncovered). → Cousin-ing: [N/A — bacterial]; *Neisseria* kin above. → Pillars: encephalitis (meningitis [D]); pneumoniae (meningococcemia/pneumonia [D]).

**The workout:**

- **The names.** *Neisseria* honors Albert Neisser, who found the gonococcus in 1879 [D]; *meningitidis* was isolated from spinal fluid by Weichselbaum in 1887 [D]. The American old names say the showing: **cerebrospinal fever** (the fever sits in brain and spine) and **spotted fever** (the purpuric spots of meningococcemia) [D]. Namology warning, his: "spotted fever" also names Rocky Mountain spotted fever — two different organisms, one American name. The name can cost the cure (his §124 sting).
- **The organism.** A diplococcus — paired spheres, his coccus clan [D]. Gram-negative, kidney-bean pairs [D]. It lives as a colonist first: roughly 1 in 10 people carry it in the nasopharynx without illness [D] — his colonization thesis wearing a lab coat.
- **The kin.** *N. gonorrhoeae* — same genus, different showing [D]. One genus, two American names, two diseases. (Informational only; his §142 territory is his.)
- **The serogroups.** A, B, C, Y, W-135 — capsule types, the same capsule logic as his pneumococcal serotype work [D]. The 1997 vaccine covered A/C/Y/W-135; **B was uncovered** [D] — the gap sat in the record until the B vaccines (Bexsero, Trumenba) arrived decades later [D].
- **The vaccines.** Menomune (MPSV4, 1981) — the plain polysaccharide [D]; Menactra/Menveo — the conjugates, polysaccharide married to a protein carrier so infants' immune systems can see it [D]. His reversology: the 2010-era conjugation trick, same as his Prevnar.
- **The pillars.** Meningitis → the **encephalitis** pillar (inflammation of the meninges, the brain's wrapping — the between, his §138) [D]. Meningococcemia and meningococcal pneumonia → the **pneumoniae** pillar [D]. One organism, two pillars — the tree's cross-branch tissue.
- **The gaps.** Fungi: [GAP] — his column. Viruses: [GAP] — his to fill.

## Entry 20 — Anthrax — *Bacillus anthracis*

**The entry, repeated verbatim from the Master List:**

> Old names: woolsorter's disease [D]. → *B. anthracis* [D]. → Fungi: [GAP]. → Bacteria: the bacterium IS the disease [D]. → Viruses: [GAP]. → Vaccines: BioThrax (AVA) — protective antigen filtrate [D]. → Serotypes: [N/A] [D]. → Cousin-ing: *B. cereus*, *B. thuringiensis* [D]. → Pillars: pneumoniae (inhalational [D]); encephalopathy (toxin-mediated [D]).

**The workout:**

- **The names.** *Anthracis* is Greek *anthrax* — **coal** [D]. The Latin names the lesion: the black eschar, coal-black dead tissue. His namology in one word: the name IS the showing. The American old name — **woolsorter's disease** — names the victim and the route (the wool trade, the spores in the hides) [D]: the American names the showing, the victim, the route (his Group 1 closing line, verbatim in spirit).
- **The organism.** A spore-forming rod [D] — the spore is the survival trick, shared with his Clostridium cousins (§122) [D]. "The bacterium IS the disease" — no separate disease-name needed; organism and showing are one [D].
- **The history.** Koch proved it: anthrax, 1876 — the work that became Koch's postulates, the root of germ theory itself [D]. This is the solid ground his "solid diseases first" stands on.
- **The showings.** Cutaneous (the coal eschar), gastrointestinal, and **inhalational** — the inhaled spores → hemorrhagic mediastinitis, the chest filling with blood [D]. Inhalational anthrax terminates on the **pneumoniae** pillar: the lung showing [D]. The toxin — protective antigen plus lethal factor plus edema factor — goes systemic and terminates on the **encephalopathy** pillar [D]. Poison doing the traveling, not the bug: his toxin thesis (§122–123, the botulinum exception) in another uniform.
- **The vaccine.** BioThrax (AVA) — an adsorbed filtrate built around the **protective antigen** [D]. The vaccine names the poison's key: immunity aimed at the toxin's door-handle. Compare his ACAM2000 note (immunity through kinship) — here, immunity through the poison's own machinery.
- **The cousins.** *B. cereus* (food poisoning) and *B. thuringiensis* (the biopesticide) [D] — same genus, different showings. The clan logic holds: one genus, many Americans.
- **The gaps.** Fungi: [GAP] — his column. Viruses: [GAP] — his to fill. Serotypes: [N/A] — capsules aren't the story here; spores and toxin are [D].
