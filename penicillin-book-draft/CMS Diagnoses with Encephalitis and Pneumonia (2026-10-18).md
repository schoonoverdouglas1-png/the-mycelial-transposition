## TOXEMIA — the honest gap [H term; no code]

**"Toxemia" is his term** for the fungal element (toxemia + meningitis = fungal, in his split) [H]. **ICD-10-CM has no "toxemia" code as such** — stated plainly, no code invented. The historical "toxemia of pregnancy" codes to O14.- (pre-eclampsia) [D]; that is a different thing entirely. What the coding record actually uses where his "toxemia" points: **B37.7** (candidal sepsis / fungemia), **B37.9** (candidiasis, unspecified), and the A41 sepsis codes for the bacterial side. The gap is marked [GAP]: his word has no coding counterpart — the record speaks sepsis and candidemia where he says toxemia.
