## Valley fever → pneumonia [D]

*Coccidioides* makes lung disease that looks like community-acquired pneumonia — and chronic fibrocavitary disease that looks like tuberculosis. The great imitator at work: the same fungus, the pneumonia showing.

## Valley fever → encephalitis/meningitis [D]

Coccidioidal meningitis is real and serious — basilar, chronic, mimicking tuberculous meningitis, almost invariably fatal untreated. The fungus reaches the brain's coverings.

## The infections that happen with all those things [D]

With the pneumonia showing: the bacterial pneumonias (*S. pneumoniae*, *Klebsiella*, *H. influenzae*), TB itself, influenza and its bacterial secondaries. With the meningitis showing: bacterial meningitis (*N. meningitidis*, *S. pneumoniae*), cryptococcal meningitis, the viral encephalitides (including Jamestown Canyon and La Crosse). The differential is the crowd valley fever walks in.
