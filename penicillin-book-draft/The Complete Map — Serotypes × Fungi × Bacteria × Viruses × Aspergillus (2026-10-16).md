## Documented edges — serotype ↔ organism

- **Staph-biofilm five:** 3, 14, 18C, 19F, 19A — mixed *S. aureus* biofilms **(documented)**
- **Pneumo → Staph:** H2O2 killing (SpxB); negative carriage association; PCV7 doubled Staph colonization **(documented)**
- **Pneumo → Aspergillus:** H2O2 fragments *A. fumigatus* biofilms **(documented)**
- **Aspergillus → pneumo:** gliotoxin inhibits pneumo, synergizes with antibiotics **(documented)**
- **Pneumo ↔ H. influenzae:** competition (H2O2, neuraminidase, arginine) + synergy (co-carriage, dual biofilms) **(documented)**
- **Pneumo ↔ M. catarrhalis:** positive co-carriage (OR 3.07); biofilm synergy **(documented)**
- **M. catarrhalis ↔ Staph:** negative association (OR 0.06) **(documented)**
- **Pneumo ↔ TB:** co-infection both directions, all 23 at species level **(documented)**
- **Candida + pneumo:** one co-infection case report **(documented)**
- **C. auris + pneumo:** one sequential case (shared risk, not interaction) **(documented)**
- **Crypto + pneumo:** two co-infection cases + one false-positive panel **(documented)**
- **Candida ↔ oral streptococci:** rich biofilm synergy — NOT pneumococcus **(documented)**
- **C. auris ↔ pneumo/serotypes:** nothing **(documented absence)**
- **Malassezia, Saccharomyces, C. glabrata ↔ pneumo:** nothing **(documented absence)**

## Documented edges — the Aspergillus relationship web

- **Aspergillus diseases:** invasive pulmonary, chronic pulmonary, aspergilloma, ABPA **(documented)**
