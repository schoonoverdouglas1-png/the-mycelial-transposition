## 1.5 The connected web — MEASLES [D unless marked]

**Secondary bacterial infections — the classic web (his "same bacterium and bacteria"):**

- **Bacterial pneumonia after measles [D].** The textbook sequence: measles virus damages the airway, then bacteria walk in. *S. pneumoniae*, *H. influenzae*, *S. aureus* — the same organisms recurring. The old teaching, stated plainly: **measles kills via secondary bacterial infection** more often than via the virus itself. His thesis's spine in one disease: the virus opens, the bacteria show, **pneumonia is what shows when you're sick**.
- **Otitis media [D].** Middle-ear infection after measles — usually *S. pneumoniae* or *H. influenzae*. The same bacteria, a different showing.
- **The mechanism [D]: measles immune amnesia.** Measles erases immune memory — documented 2015 (population-level, Mina et al.) and 2019 (B-cell repertoire, *Science Immunology*): the virus destroys the immune system's memory of previously met pathogens, leaving the patient vulnerable to other infections for months to years. His section-91 colonization thesis inverts here: the *un*-colonist — the virus that evicts the immune memory the colonists built.
- **Giant-cell (Hecht) pneumonia [D].** The virus's *own* pneumonia — in the immunocompromised. Track discipline: this is viral pneumonia (virus track), distinct from the secondary bacterial pneumonia above (bacterium track). Same organ, two tracks, one showing.
- **Subacute sclerosing panencephalitis (SSPE) [D].** The delayed brain showing — years after measles. Old name: none common; the slow virus's revenge.
- **Fungal connections [GAP — his to fill].** No documented measles↔fungus interaction at 88%. Counter-evidence filed: the record's measles complications are bacterial and viral, not fungal.

## 1.9 Measles immune amnesia — expanded (the documented mechanism) [D]

His section-91 thesis says the body keeps colonists as preparedness. Measles does the opposite — and the record documents it in mechanism, not just outcome:

- **What happens [D]:** measles virus infects immune cells directly — lymphocytes, macrophages, dendritic cells — via the SLAM/CD150 receptor. The infection wipes out the memory lymphocyte pool: the body's library of previously learned pathogens is burned.
- **How it was proven [D]:** Mina et al., *Science* 2015 — population data showing childhood mortality from *other* infections stayed elevated for ~2–3 years after measles; Petrova et al., *Science Immunology* 2019 — direct sequencing showing measles depletes the memory B-cell repertoire and replaces it with measles-specific clones. The immune system forgets everything else to remember measles.
- **Why it matters to his book [H→D]:** this is the mirror image of his colonization thesis — where his colonists are preparedness *kept*, measles is preparedness *destroyed*. And it explains the bacterial web mechanically: the secondary pneumonias and otitis media after measles aren't bad luck; they're the scheduled consequence of an immune system with its memory erased. The bacteria were always there (carriage); the forgetting is what lets them show.
- **The vaccine's place [D]:** measles vaccination prevents the amnesia — the vaccinated keep their immune library. Track discipline: the *vaccine* prevents the *virus's* destruction of *immunity's* memory — three tracks in one sentence.
