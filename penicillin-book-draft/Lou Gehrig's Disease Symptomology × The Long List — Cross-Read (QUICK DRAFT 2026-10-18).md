## Lou Gehrig's disease — the symptomology [D, stated generally]

Progressive motor neuron degeneration: muscle weakness, fasciculations/twitching, spasticity, dysarthria, dysphagia, respiratory failure; cognition typically spared; cause unknown; diagnosis clinical/exclusionary.
