## How to read the rows

- **Sort:** most complete information first, narrowing to the least — the connection marked at every row, even the thin end, per his order. Completeness score = documented cells out of 6 (fungi / bacteria / viruses / vaccines / serotypes / viral cousin-ing). [GAP] scores 0; [N/A] with a stated reason scores 1 (it is information).
- **Name-type** per row: same / translated / eponym / geographic / different — how the American name relates to the Latin.
- **Four-pillar termination** per row: which of his pillars the disease terminates at — tuberculosis, pneumoniae, encephalitis, encephalopathy — or [GAP] where the record is silent.
- **Tier tags** on every claim: [D] documented · [H] his · [H→D] his with a documented foothold · [GAP] empty, his to fill · [N/A] not applicable, reason given.
- **Six tracks** never collapsed: vaccine ≠ virus ≠ disease ≠ illness ≠ sickness ≠ infection.
