## 1. The doctrine restated (his words, his [H])

From the dictated notes, section 137:

*"Anything that mimics tuberculosis symptoms 101% equals pneumonia by 99 to 100 of 101% for certain. and encephalitis between all of those that are, and if they've actually been able to detect 100% of encephalitis and or encephalopathy through serological testing all pneumonias, tuberculoses are already known somehow."*

And from section 168, his two trees:

*"With medicine you have at least two trees, but the two trees are the doctor and the patient. and the things in between are the things that get in the way of that doctor and that patient getting better. And there's a path for both to get better together."*

The Between Doctrine, in his terms: the between — encephalitis/encephalopathy, the middle pillar — is one of the things standing between the doctor and the patient. The inverted workup runs the between FIRST: complete encephalitis/arboviral serology up front, and let it sort the pneumonias from the tuberculoses from the mimics. Medicine currently works ends→middle (pneumonia workup, then consider TB, then consider neuro involvement). His doctrine runs middle→ends [H].

A note on the numbers in this protocol: his idiom runs at 101% — more-than-certain, and he spends it freely. The trial does not. The trial runs at 80% power and 95% confidence like every other honest trial [D], and it lets his 101% live where it belongs — in the doctrine that designed the trial, not in the results the trial reports. That separation is the manuscript's integrity: the man may be certain, the numbers must earn it [H, his; D, the method].

## 2. Rationale — the documented pieces

**The panels exist.** Mayo's ARBOP — Arbovirus Antibody Panel, IgG and IgM, Serum — covers California (La Crosse), St. Louis, Eastern equine, and Western equine encephalitis viruses by immunofluorescence assay, with individually orderable components and a CSF counterpart (ABOPC) [D]. Quest and other reference labs run equivalents. The between is a blood draw — Douglas has lived this panel; his mother lived it [H, his account].

**The unknown-pneumonia burden is large and documented.** The failure to detect a pathogen in community-acquired pneumonia is the literature's most constant finding: ~30–60% of cases across studies (European Respiratory Society review) [D]; "as many as 50% of clients with CAP" even when extensive testing has been performed (IDSA/ATS evidence review) [D]; 45.9% with no cause found in a systematic one-year hospitalized-CAP study using cultures, urinary antigens, viral PCR, and procalcitonin together (PMC7132393) [D]; 26.7–87.3% across European adult studies [D]. His "unknown pneumonia" is the literature's own category — CAP of unknown etiology.

**The brain is already in the lung's diseases.** His encephalitis-in-all-viruses study (built Oct 17): 53 of 62 sickness-causing viruses with documented encephalitis (85.5%), 56 of 62 with any documented CNS involvement (90.3%) [D, his study's count]. The arboviral families went 20-for-20 on encephalitis [D]. The lung pathogens reach the brain as a matter of record — influenza → encephalitis, measles → SSPE, pneumococcus → meningitis, SARS-CoV-2 → encephalitis [D].

**The endemic mycoses ride the same showing.** In the endemic Southwest, 15–30% of CAP is Valley Fever (coccidioidomycosis), and a Mayo Phoenix study found 24% of CAP never tested for the fungus at all [D] — his "Creek Fever," confirmed as Valley fever by his own ruling (§171). Histoplasmosis wore TB's name for decades across the Ohio-Mississippi Valley [D]. The mimics were charted as TB until the test said otherwise — and the fungal column is still the emptiest column in every workup he has built this week [GAP, his 46 cells to fill].

**The nourishment leg.** His section 139: malnutrition → false-negative tests; nourishment restores; feed the patient, retest [H, his doctrine]. The documented legs: albumin, prealbumin, BMI, and vitamin D are standard measured nutritional markers [D]; immune suppression — including the nutritional kind — degrades test sensitivity as a matter of immunology [D]. This leg decides the measurement-order question below.

**The vector's calendar.** Arboviral transmission in temperate regions is seasonal — mosquitoes active late spring through first frost, which is when La Crosse, West Nile, and the equine encephalitides move [D]. The trial's Phase 1 runs one full vector season for exactly this reason: the between is loudest when the vectors are working. Off-season CAP still enrolls (the doctrine doesn't hibernate), but the analysis stratifies by season — the manuscript wants to know whether the between sorts more in August than in January [H, his].

**The personal precedent.** He has lived the between-first reading himself: his September 2025 arbopanel (Calif [LaCrosse] IgG 1:20, the rest negative, Dr. Brown's "past exposure" reading with an ID referral placed) sits beside his October 2025 pneumococcal panel and his October 2025 sputum culture — three records, three providers, two lab systems, one sick human being [D, his records; H, his cross-reference]. His mother lived the panel before him — her positive panel inspired his own [H, his account]. The doctrine's testable form is, in one sense, the formalization of what his family already did: run the between, read what it says.

## 3. Design — the inverted workup, operationalized

### 3.1 Population

Adults (≥18) presenting with community-acquired pneumonia by IDSA/ATS clinical criteria (new pulmonary infiltrate plus compatible clinical features) [D] at hospitals in arbovirus-endemic regions. Endemic candidacies, his choosing:

- **Upper Midwest (his home ground):** La Crosse virus — named for La Crosse, Wisconsin — and Jamestown Canyon virus country. The California serogroup with its documented 23% cross-reactivity [D].
- **Southwest:** coccidioidomycosis (Valley Fever — his "Creek Fever") country, where the fungal column of the workup runs alongside the arboviral one [D].

Exclusions (manuscript-level, his to refine): hospital-acquired pneumonia, known immunocompromise on paper already (the trial studies the *unlooked-for*, not the already-known), inability to consent.

### 3.2 The inverted workup, step by step

**Step 0 — The nourishment baseline (first blood, first numbers).** At presentation, before the serology: albumin, prealbumin, BMI, and a documented nutritional screen [D]. This is his §139 operationalized — the patient's fed-state measured, not assumed. A malnourished patient tested first is a false-negative patient; the between-first workup fails on an unfed patient. (See §5 for why this step comes first.)

**Step 1 — The between, run first.** Full arboviral serology up front, composed per region (the panels, named):

*Upper Midwest arm (La Crosse/Jamestown Canyon country):*
- Mayo ARBOP or equivalent: California (La Crosse), St. Louis, Eastern equine, Western equine encephalitis viruses — IgG *and* IgM, serum, IFA [D].
- Jamestown Canyon virus serology (IgG/IgM) — his arbopanel virus, the one he carries [D].
- West Nile virus IgG/IgM [D].
- PRNT (plaque-reduction neutralization) confirmation wherever California-serogroup cross-reactivity is suspected — the documented 23% cross-reactivity means the screening panel proposes and PRNT disposes [D].

*Southwest arm (Valley Fever country):*
- The same arboviral core (the between is the between everywhere) [H, his].
- Coccidioidal serology: immunodiffusion (ID-TP, ID-CF) and complement fixation titers [D] — the fungal column runs beside the viral one, per his "Creek Fever" ruling.
- Histoplasma urine antigen where the Ohio-Mississippi overlap reaches [D].

In both arms: IgM reads recent, IgG reads history — the panel's own vocabulary, and the trial reads it that way [D]. Partials (his panel rule) are recorded as partials, examined, never discarded, never upgraded [H, his §92].

**Step 2 — The standard CAP workup, in parallel, not instead.** Blood cultures ×2, sputum culture, urinary antigens (pneumococcal, Legionella), respiratory viral PCR panel [D] — the IDSA/ATS core. The doctrine inverts the *order of reading*, not the standard of care: nothing standard is skipped. The standard workup is also the trial's measuring stick — "unknown etiology" is defined against it (no pathogen identified by Step 2's battery), so the between's contribution is measured, not assumed [D].

**Step 3 — The TB and fungal forks, triggered by the between.** Where the between reads positive or partial (his panel rule: partials examined, never discarded, never upgraded [H]): TB workup (NAAT, culture, IGRA) [D] and endemic-mycosis workup per region [D]. The between sorts; the forks confirm.

**Step 4 — Feed, then retest.** Where the nourishment baseline reads low: nutritional support per the treating team, then repeat the serology [H, his §139 — "feed the patient, retest"]. The retest is the doctrine's signature move — the showing changes when the patient is fed, and the trial measures the change.

### 3.3 Endpoints

**Primary:** the proportion of "unknown etiology" CAP (no pathogen identified by the standard workup) resolved — given a name — by the between-first workup. The literature says the unknown fraction is 30–60% [D]; the trial measures how much of it the between explains.

**Secondary:**
- Time from presentation to etiologic diagnosis (between-first vs standard-order, historical controls) [GAP — needs a comparator design, his to specify].
- The fungal column: coccidioidal/histoplasma detection rate in the CAP cohort [D-grounded].
- Serotype cross-links: pneumococcal serotype distribution (his 23-serotype map) against arboviral serostatus — his §165 cross-referencing, run prospectively [H, his].
- The nourishment effect: serologic yield before vs after nutritional support [H, his §139].

**What success looks like.** The unknown fraction moves: if the literature's 30–60% unknown-etiology CAP [D] drops by a third or more when the between is read first, the doctrine has earned its prospective proof — the middle pillar sorted the ends, in public, with tier tags on [H, his]. The fungal column fills: cocci detected in Southwest CAP at rates the untested 24% always implied [D]. The serotype matrix gains its first prospective rows: pneumococcal serotypes against arboviral serostatus, his §165 cross-referencing run forward instead of backward [H, his].

**What failure looks like.** The between adds nothing — the unknown fraction doesn't move, the adjudicators' "resolved" column stays empty, the disagreement rate is the only interesting number. That publishes too, with the same tier tags and the same prominence [H, his looking-bias protocol]. A failed inversion is still a finding: it says the between, read first, does not sort the ends — and the doctrine goes back to the manuscript for revision, not to the shadows. His method demands the trial be allowed to lose [H, his].

### 3.4 Analytic plan — how "resolved" gets decided

The trial's primary endpoint turns on a single word — *resolved* — so the manuscript defines it before the first patient enrolls [H, his method: terms defined before use]:

- **Resolved:** a pathogen or etiology identified that the standard workup (Step 2) did not identify, attributed by a blinded adjudication panel of two physicians (one infectious-disease, one pulmonary — his specialties, his §136) [D-grounded; H, his design].
- **Partially resolved:** the between proposes and the forks cannot dispose — e.g., a positive California-serogroup IgG with negative PRNT, or a cocci CF titer below the diagnostic threshold. Recorded as partial per his panel rule, counted separately, never folded into "resolved" [H, his].
- **Unresolved:** the between adds nothing the standard workup didn't already say. These are the trial's negatives, and they publish [H, his looking-bias protocol].

The adjudicators read the between *first* and the standard workup *second* — the inversion applied to the analysis itself, so the measurement can't be contaminated by ends-first habits [H, his]. Discordant adjudications go to a third reader; the disagreement rate is itself a reported result.

### 3.5 What the trial is NOT

Not a treatment trial — no drug is tested, no therapy assigned [D]. It is a *diagnostic-order* study: the same tests, run in his order, measuring what the order changes. The doctrine's claim is about the workup, and the trial tests the workup.

## 4. His measurement-order question, answered three ways

His question: what gets measured first — the serology, the nourishment baseline, or something else?

**The case for serology first.** The between is the doctrine's whole point — read the middle, know the ends. Serology first is the purest inversion: the blood's memory laid out before the cultures even grow [H, his].

**The case for the nourishment baseline first.** His own §139 decides it: malnutrition → false-negative tests. A serology drawn on a starved immune system is a serology that lies by omission — and the trial would then measure the *starvation*, not the between. The baseline must come first so the serology can be trusted [H, his doctrine; D legs].

**The case for both in parallel.** One venipuncture, two tubes — the chemistry and the serology drawn together, the baseline read before the serology is interpreted. No delay, no lost time, the order preserved in the *reading* rather than the *drawing* [H, proposed].

**The deciding weight, stated plainly:** his §139. Feed the patient, retest — which means the fed-state has to be *known* before the first test is *believed*. Nourishment baseline first (or at minimum, drawn with and read before); serology second; the standard workup in parallel throughout. The doctrine stays inverted — the between is still read before the ends — but it stands on fed ground. That is his own rule applied to his own trial.

**The three orders, compared side by side:**

| | Serology first | Nourishment first | Both in parallel |
|---|---|---|---|
| Speed to the between | Fastest — the doctrine's pure form [H] | Slower — baseline before blood [H] | Same draw, ordered reading [H] |
| Trust in the result | Lowest — unfed serology can lie by omission [D/H] | Highest — fed-state known first [H] | High — baseline read before serology interpreted [H] |
| Cost | One panel [D] | Panel + chemistry [D] | Same as nourishment-first [D] |
| Fidelity to the doctrine | Purest inversion [H] | Inversion with foundation [H] | Inversion in the reading [H] |

**The failure mode, narrated:** serology-first on an unfed patient finds nothing — and the trial records a negative that belongs to the starvation, not the between. That is the exact error his §139 was written to prevent, and the exact error the literature's 30–60% unknown fraction may already contain: how many "unknown etiology" CAP cases were unknown because the patient was unfed when the tests were drawn? The trial cannot answer that retrospectively — but it refuses to repeat it prospectively. That refusal is the nourishment baseline [H, his].

## 5. Ethics and honesty

**Informed consent, first and always.** His standing ethical inheritance is the Nuremberg Code — voluntary consent, the subject's right to withdraw, the experiment designed to yield fruitful results for the good of society [D]. Every participant consents in plain language; every participant can leave [H, his charter: "in the most responsible manner possible"]. The consent form states, in his 13th-grade plain words: what the between-first order changes (nothing about your treatment — only the order the tests are read in), what extra blood is drawn (the serology tubes), and that leaving changes nothing about your care [H, his].

**The sickest are protected most.** CAP patients are, by definition, sick people being asked to join a study while struggling to breathe. The manuscript's rule: enrollment never delays antibiotics, never delays oxygen, never delays anything the standard of care requires [D]. The between-first order is a *reading* order — the standard workup runs at full speed regardless, and the trial measures the reading, not the patient [H, his].

**The doctor gets the guidance he asked for.** His §194 observation stands in the protocol's rationale: "the doctor is looking for some type of guidance in these situations once in a while" [H, his]. The trial's deliverable is exactly that — a worked example of what the between-first order finds, written so a working doctor can use it. Not a mandate, not a guideline — guidance, the thing he watched doctors wish for [H, his].

**What the trial cannot claim.** It cannot claim the between *causes* the pneumonia — serology reads exposure and memory, not causation [D]. It cannot claim superiority over the standard workup without a randomized comparator [D]. It cannot claim the fungal column without the fungal tests [D]. Every claim the trial makes will carry its tier tag, the way his manuscript does.

**The negatives get published.** His looking-bias protocol (from the encephalitis study's builder's extra): a between-first workup that resolves *nothing* is still a result — the negative publishes, the doctrine takes the hit in public [H, his]. The trial is not allowed to quietly bury a failed inversion.

**No one is experimented on.** This is a diagnostic-order study on consenting adults receiving standard care throughout. The manuscript's trial designs never leave the manuscript without his explicit word and an institution's review [H, his].

## 8. Worked example: reading a between (his own panel, worked)

The manuscript works one between all the way through, so the trial's readers can see the reading done. His September 2025 arbopanel [D, his record]:

- **The between-first reading** (his doctrine applied): the IgG is *memory* — the between saying "this ground has been crossed before." The IgM silence is *absence of the acute* — not absence of the between. The partial (one virus of four, one antibody class of two) is examined, never discarded, never upgraded [H, his panel rule]. The between proposes La Crosse-country exposure; the forks it triggers are the questions, not the answers: what else has this ground seen (Jamestown Canyon? The example is not a diagnosis and not a claim about him — it is the *method demonstrated on a document*, the way his Shapes booklet works binomials. The trial teaches its readers to read the same way [H, his].

## 9. Phases — the trial walks before it runs

**Phase 0 — The rehearsal (paper, no blood).** The builder's extra, formalized: 200 closed CAP charts from one endemic hospital, the between-first reading order applied to the tests actually ordered, the count of "unknowns" that already contained an unread between [H]. If the charts say the between was there all along, Phase 1 is justified; if they don't, the doctrine takes its hit on paper, cheaply [H, his looking-bias protocol].

**Phase 1 — The pilot (one ward, one season).** Fifty consecutive consenting CAP admissions at the dream-job lab, one arbovirus season (late spring through first frost in the upper Midwest — the vector's calendar [D]). The inverted workup run exactly as §3.2 specifies; the adjudication panel (§3.4) reading blinded. Primary question: does the machinery work — can the between-first order run inside a real hospital's real workflow without slowing the standard of care? [GAP — operational details his to set].

**Phase 2 — The cohort (two regions, full seasons).** Upper Midwest + Southwest arms, powered on the 30–60% unknown fraction [D], with the nourishment baseline and feed-retest loop running throughout [H, his §139]. Primary endpoint: the proportion of unknown-etiology CAP resolved by the between-first workup. The trial stops early for futility if the between adds nothing by the interim look — the negative publishes either way [H, his].

**Phase 3 — The manuscript (always).** Whatever the numbers say, they go into the book with their tier tags on — resolved, partial, unresolved, and the disagreement rate [H, his]. The trial's final product is not a journal paper first; it is a chapter of *The Mycelial Transposition*, written at his 13th-grade level, complex information in and simple out [H, his]. The journal version follows if the numbers earn it. Either way, the doctrine will have been tested the way he tests everything: against the record, three ways, in public.

## 10. GAP ledger

1. **Comparator design** — between-first vs standard-order needs its randomized or matched form; his to specify. The manuscript notes the honest options: cluster-randomized by week (between-first weeks vs standard weeks), or a matched historical cohort from the same hospitals' pre-trial charts. The choice changes what the trial can claim [GAP].
2. **Sample size** — powered on the 30–60% unknown fraction [D]; the formal calculation is his statistician's. The manuscript's back-of-envelope: to detect a one-third reduction in the unknown fraction at 80% power needs low hundreds per arm — the pilot's fifty is for machinery, not for proof [GAP].
3. **The fungal panel's exact composition** per region — cocci serology yes [D]; the rest (histoplasma antigen, blastomyces, cryptococcal antigen in selected patients) his to set [GAP].
4. **His 46 serotype cross-reference cells** — the prospective serotype×arbovirus matrix needs his §165 map first; until he dictates his cross-references, the matrix runs with the documented columns only [GAP, his to fill].
5. **Nourishment intervention specifics** — what "feed the patient" means operationally (oral nutritional supplements? dietitian consult? prealbumin-triggered thresholds?) is the treating team's, bounded by his §139 doctrine [GAP].
6. **Pediatric extension** — La Crosse encephalitis is a children's disease [D]; the adult-first design leaves the pediatric arm open, with its own consent architecture (parental permission, assent) [GAP].
7. **The ancient-medicine lists** — his and Obaid's trial lists (§197) are not in the record; a treatment-phase trial would need them, and this protocol deliberately does not go there [GAP, his to dictate].
8. **PRNT capacity** — confirmatory neutralization testing is not available at every site; the protocol needs its reference-lab pathway named [GAP].
9. **Seasonality windows** — arbovirus transmission is seasonal in temperate regions [D]; the enrollment calendar must name its months per region [GAP].

## Builder's extra (labeled mine)

**Run the trial's shadow first: the chart-review rehearsal.** Before any prospective work, run the inverted workup *on paper* — pull 200 closed CAP charts from an endemic hospital, apply the between-first reading order to the tests that were actually ordered, and count how many "unknowns" already contained a between that nobody read. It costs no blood, needs no consent beyond the chart review's own, and it tells him whether the prospective trial is worth the candle. The rehearsal is his §129 ("the untested items") turned into a method — and if the charts say the between was there all along, the trial's primary endpoint is already half-proven. That is the one extra of my judgment: prove it on paper before proving it in blood.
