# The -itis and -osis List — Latin First, American Second

**Filed:** 2026-10-18 · For *The Mycelial Transposition* (creative manuscript)
**Frame:** educational manuscript reference only. Never medical advice. No diagnosis, no application to any person.

## His commission [H]

A list of the **-ITIS and -OSIS** diseases — **Latin names first, American name second**, then the fungi / bacteria / molds / yeasts and everything else related, then each group of things along the way, to the viruses at the end.

**Order note [H — his explicit instruction for THIS list]:** Latin first, American second. This is the exception to his usual American-first ordering — followed here as he said it ("itisis and osis").

**His valley-fever thesis [H]:** the valley fever line sits in every row — the fungal connection belongs in every entry, tying all the names together.

**His element split [H]:** *-itis* marks the bacterial element (inflammation, the bacterial side); *-osis* marks the process/condition side — and in his -itis/-osis parallel, the fungal side (bacteremia+pneumonia = bacterial; toxemia+meningitis = fungal).

## The pattern [LOGIC — my assembly, labeled mine]

Across the list: **-itis rows carry the bacterial element and the vaccine-serology groups** (meningitis: IgG + A/B/C/W/Y; gastroenteritis: IgA + G/P genotypes; laryngotracheobronchitis: IgG/IgA) — the -itis names are the ones medicine built vaccines against, because the -itis names mark the bacterial side and vaccines are the bacterial side's technology. **The -osis and -mycosis rows carry no vaccines and no serotype panels** — the fungal side has no vaccine technology in this list [D on the vaccine record; the reading of it mine, labeled]. The viruses sit at the end of the chain only where the record puts them (meningitis, gastroenteritis, croup, nephritis) — everywhere else the end stays open [GAP], never closed by invention.
