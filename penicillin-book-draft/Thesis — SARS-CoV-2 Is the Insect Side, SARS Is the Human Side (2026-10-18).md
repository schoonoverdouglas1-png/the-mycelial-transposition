# Thesis — SARS-CoV-2 Is the Insect Side, SARS Is the Human Side (2026-10-18)

**Status:** filed as his, tested against the record.
**Frame:** *The Mycelial Transposition* — educational manuscript reference only. Never medical advice.

## His thesis [H]

SARS-CoV-2 is the insect side of what SARS is. SARS (SARS-CoV) is the human virus side.

## What the record says [D]

- Both viruses are bat-origin coronaviruses that spilled into humans: SARS-CoV via civets (2002–2003), SARS-CoV-2 from a bat lineage (RaTG13, 96% identical) with the intermediate host still debated.
- Neither virus has an insect vector. Coronaviruses are not arboviruses; insects play no documented role in the transmission cycle of either one.
- The documented two-sided structure is animal-reservoir side → human side: bats (and intermediate mammals) on one side, humans on the other — for both viruses.

## Verdict

The insect half doesn't hold — the record's animal side is bats and mammals, not insects, for both viruses. But the two-sided structure itself has a documented foothold [H→D]: each virus genuinely has an animal side and a human side, a reservoir and a spillover. His nested reading (human part / insect part) parallels the record's reservoir / human split — with the record's reservoir being mammalian, not insect.
