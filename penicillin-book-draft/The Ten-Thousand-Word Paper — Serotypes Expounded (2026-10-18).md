## Structured Abstract

**Background.** Serotype-specific anti-capsular-polysaccharide IgG panels (e.g., Mayo PN23M, 23 serotypes) measure immune memory against *Streptococcus pneumoniae* capsules, but the clinical meaning of individual serotype values is interpreted by clinicians, not by the panel itself. The documented literature attaches to each serotype a distinct profile: vaccine membership across four product generations, disease associations, resistance clustering, biofilm architecture, co-pathogen interactions, and virus-amplified virulence.

**Objective.** To expound all 23 panel serotypes against the full documented record — vaccines, diseases, resistance, biofilms, cocci and concurrences, virus couplings, disease-group memberships — within a classified-evidence framework, and to organize the findings into a diagnostic-use classification.

**Methods.** Eight prior workups were condensed. Every claim was classified by tier tag ([D]/[H]/[H→D]/[LOGIC]/[GAP]). Exclusion criteria: no interpretation of the subject's personal panel values, no treatment recommendations, no diagnostic conclusions about any person.

**Results.** All 23 serotypes were expounded. Key findings: 10 of 23 carry documented resistance associations [D]; the classic six resistant serotypes (6B, 9V, 14, 19A, 19F, 23F) plus 11A, 33F, 15B, and 8 (watchlisted); serotype 3 is the strongest measured biofilm former with PSSP 100% — capsule biology, not resistance, drives its vaccine breakthrough [D]; 19A's capsular switch from the MDR 19F clone Taiwan19F-ST236 is the fully documented end-to-end chain of vaccine-driven replacement [D]; serotype 4 shows the largest measured flu-driven mortality amplification (0%→79%) [D]; the mixed *S. aureus* biofilm literature is serotype-resolved for only 5 of 23 (3, 14, 18C, 19F, 19A) [D]; 23F carries the second-strongest measured biofilm architecture among resistant serotypes with no documented staph pairing [GAP]. Ten overlapping disease groups were derived for diagnostic use; 14 and 23F occupy seven groups each [D].

**Conclusions.** The serotype number is the stable identifier across vaccine generations, resistance evolution, and disease nomenclature; vaccine compositions, drug susceptibilities, and disease names rotate around it. The classified framework separates documented findings from the subject's theses at every step. Limitations: per-serotype data are thin for archive serotypes (2, 17F, 20), serotype-specific virus couplings are absent for eleven serotypes, and the per-serotype fungal/yeast column is entirely empty [GAP].

## Introduction

*Streptococcus pneumoniae* is classified into more than 100 serotypes by capsular polysaccharide chemistry; the serotype number is a capsule-chemistry classification, not a species distinction [D]. Licensed pneumococcal vaccines are formulated against subsets of these capsules: the 23-valent polysaccharide vaccine PPSV23 (1983) and the conjugate vaccines PCV15 (2021), PCV20 (2021/2023), and PCV21 (2024), each carrying a different serotype list [D]. Serotype-specific IgG panels measure antibodies against the 23 PPSV23 capsules; the laboratory's acceptability criteria are written around pre- and post-vaccination comparisons, and the laboratory's own text states that values at or above the threshold are "consistent with an immune response, but are not necessarily sufficient to provide protection against infection" [D].

The clinical literature documents that serotypes differ biologically, not merely numerically. Resistance clusters by serotype through altered mosaic penicillin-binding proteins [D]; capsular switching allows resistant clones to change serotype identity while retaining resistance genes — documented end-to-end for the 19F→19A transition [D]; biofilm architecture differs measurably by serotype [D — Allegrucci et al. 2006]; influenza amplifies pneumococcal virulence through at least three documented mechanisms [D]; and pneumococcus–*Staphylococcus aureus* interactions (H₂O₂ warfare, dual-species biofilms, dispersion modulation under viral conditions) are documented at species resolution with serotype-resolved data for only a minority of serotypes [D].

This paper expounds each of the 23 panel serotypes against that literature. It does not interpret any individual's results and makes no clinical recommendations. Its contribution is organizational: a classified, serotype-resolved synthesis with a diagnostic-use grouping system.

### Serotype 3 — his value: <0.1

**Vaccines:** all four — PPSV23, PCV15, PCV20, PCV21 [D]. **Diseases:** the persister. Adult pneumonia's most prevalent strain in an 18-country adult review; invasive disease; childhood empyema (76.4% of Australian cases despite vaccination); meningitis; otitis media [D]. His cousin-ing [H] puts *Coccidioides* — the fungus named for its roundness — beside it; the documented fact under the cousin-ing is only that capsule chemistry defines the serotype [D]. **Virus couplings:** with influenza (A66.1), vaccine protection collapsed to 30–40% survival [D] — the virus unravels the defense. **Disease groups:** Meningitis, Pneumonia, Otitis, Bacteremia, Outbreak, Persister [D]. **Diagram groupings:** Persisters, Biofilm-Tested Five [H — AI-proposed]. **[LOGIC] — three defeats at once.** Named in all four vaccines yet persists at pre-vaccine incidence; sheds its capsule so antibodies cannot grip; builds the strongest biofilms measured. ### 2. The vaccine compositions as a dated record of emergencies [D]

Read the four compositions in date order and you read the history of what frightened the field. **PPSV23 (1983)** is the archive: all 23, including the never-conjugated 2 and 20 [D]. **PCV7 (2000): 4, 6B, 9V, 14, 18C, 19F, 23F** → disease names: otitis media, meningitis, carriage, pneumonia — the children's set: ears, brains, and the nose they live in [D]. **PCV13 additions (1, 5, 7F, 3, 6A, 19A)** → epidemic meningitis (1, 5), the persister (3), the replacement (19A), vaccine failure (3, 7F, 19A), adult pneumonia (3) [D]. **PCV15 additions (22F, 33F)** → adult IPD, pediatric replacement deaths [D]. **PCV20 additions (8, 10A, 11A, 12F, 15B)** → adult severe IPD, outbreaks, pediatric severe IPD, the armed replacement (15B) [D]. **PCV21 (2024)** → adult severe disease plus the orphan rescue (9N, 17F) [D].

[LOGIC] — the fossil record. Each generation's serotype list was assembled in response to the failures of the previous one — 19A after PCV7, 33F after PCV13. Therefore: 2000 = children's ears and meningitis; 2010 = the replacement and the persister; 2021 = adult invasive disease; 2024 = adult severe disease. The disease names moved first; the capsule numbers followed. The vaccines are named for the organism ("pneumococcal"), never for a disease — but their contents are named for the emergencies. The disease names of vaccines are this list.
