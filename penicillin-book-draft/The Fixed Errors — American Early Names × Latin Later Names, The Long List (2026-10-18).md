# The Fixed Errors — American Early Names × Latin Later Names, The Long List

**Filed:** 2026-10-18 · For *The Mycelial Transposition* (creative manuscript)
**Frame:** educational manuscript reference only. Never medical advice. No diagnosis.

## The fix [H — his thesis]

Things must be combined in exactness to what they are, like the KLB situation: the **American early name KEPT** — because American names encompass pretty much all of the symptomology and physiology — **plus the Latin later name KEPT** — because the Latin gives the taxonomy. Both stand. Renaming by addition, never by replacement. He believes AI can do this — so the list is as long as the research supports.

**Column order:** # | American early name | Fixed form (both kept) | Latin later name | What it encompassed (symptoms) | Bacterial connections (list) | Fungal / yeast / mold connections (list).

**His thesis on the two new columns [H]:** connecting fungi and bacteria to every entry automatically connects meningitis and pneumonia to all those things.
