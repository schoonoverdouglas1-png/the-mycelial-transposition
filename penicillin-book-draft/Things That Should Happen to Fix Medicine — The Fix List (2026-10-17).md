## Fix 1 — A lab doctor over every test (his dictation, Oct 17)

His words: *"Laboratory medicine has to have a lab doctor dedicated to making certain every fucking test is activated properly and has a full reflexology done so there's no waste in blood or in testing you know to help the patient and save ultimately save Medicare and Medicaid money."*

- **The fix:** laboratory medicine gets a **dedicated lab doctor** whose job is making certain **every test is ordered properly and carries full reflex testing** — the right test, activated correctly, with automatic follow-through to confirmatory/reflex testing where indicated.
