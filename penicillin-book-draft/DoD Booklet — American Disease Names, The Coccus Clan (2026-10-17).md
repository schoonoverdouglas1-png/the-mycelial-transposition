## 3.0 THE COCCUS CLAN — ORDER OF BATTLE

The showing is single; the happening is plural.

**3.3** His section-81 doctrine governs the reading [H]: medicine looks at the coccus, not the disease. The proper order is **symptoms → coccus → treatment last**. The documented foothold: asymptomatic bacteriuria — the coccus in the urine with no illness — is **not treated** by guideline [D]. The organism without the showing is not the disease.

**3.4 THE SIMULTANEITY, EXPANDED** (his section-80 [H], with the record's muster). The nasopharynx at any moment holds: pneumococcus (carriage serotypes 6B, 19F, 23F chiefly), *S. aureus* (in ~30% of noses), nontypeable *H. influenzae*, *M. catarrhalis* — plus the viral visitors (rhinovirus, RSV) [D — serotype map, Part 2]. The organism is named for its shape AND its color. His section-82 thesis stands on the binomial itself: the same staph shows different coloring by disease balance and immune response — and the founders wrote the color into the name.

**4.3 THE MEMBERS.**

**4.3.1 *Staphylococcus aureus* — golden staph** [D]. Coagulase-positive. The clan chief. Lives in the nose of ~30% of people without harm [D] — his section-91 colonization thesis wearing a lab coat: the colonist kept as preparedness.

**4.3.2 *Staphylococcus epidermidis* — skin staph** [D]. Coagulase-negative. Skin commensal; the device infecter — catheters, joints, valves.

**4.3.3 *Staphylococcus saprophyticus* — UTI staph** [D]. Coagulase-negative. Urinary infections in young women.

Contact precautions; nasal screening on admission.

**4.7 LABORATORY CONNECTIONS** [D]:

The fungal lung diseases were tuberculosis's great mimics: **histoplasmosis** (the Ohio-Mississippi Valley misdiagnosed-as-TB classic), **coccidioidomycosis** (valley fever), **blastomycosis**, **chronic pulmonary aspergillosis** — all presenting as TB-like pulmonary disease: cough, fever, night sweats, cavities. The old doctors told them apart by geography, skin tests, and cultures; often they didn't. A fungal panel riding alongside TB testing **is** the differential diagnosis as TB was known — the test's fungal searches map the diseases TB had to be told apart from. His question — is the panel the encompassment of what TB was known to be when invented — answers itself: the panel encompasses what TB was known *against*. *"Tuberculosis pneumoniae both"* — his pairing, filed: the two old lung showings, one panel.
