## His thesis [H]

## Honest lines (do not drop)

- **Foothold [H→D]:** allergy IS an immune response — IgE-mediated hypersensitivity, mast cells, histamine, the whole apparatus is immunity in action. Generalizing from allergy to immunity starts on documented ground: the machinery overlaps (antibodies, immune cells, inflammatory mediators).
- **Seam [D]:** standard immunology also counts non-"allergic" arms — innate immunity (phagocytosis, complement, NK cells), cell-mediated immunity (T cells) — which are not described as allergic responses in the literature. His "ultimately" is doing the work of folding those arms into the allergy frame; that folding is his thesis, his to evidence.
- **The proteins thread [H→D]:** his "between tissues and proteins" parallels the antigen–antibody interaction at the center of adaptive immunity [D] — the thesis names a real interface.
