The 23 Serotypes — Vaccines, Chemistries, Diagnoses

An educational pictogram built from the vaccines' own information: which serotypes each vaccine carries, how the 23 group by documented behavior, and which diagnoses each group maps to.

How to read it: tap a vaccine button to light up only the serotypes it contains. Serotypes appear in more than one group — that overlap is real, not an error. Groupings are AI-proposed from the documented record, with the reasoning shown on each card.

1 · The four vaccines

Compositions verified against manufacturer and AAP listings.

PPSV23 · 1983 · plain polysaccharide

23 serotypes — the full set. Unconjugated sugar; no carrier protein.

PCV15 · 2021 · conjugate

15 serotypes — sugars linked to CRM197 carrier protein.

PCV20 · 2021/23 · conjugate

20 serotypes — PCV15's set plus 8, 10A, 11A, 12F, 15B.

PCV21 · 2024 · conjugate

21 serotypes — adult-focused; rescues orphans 9N & 17F. Carries 20A, not 20.

2 · The 23, grouped by documented behavior

AI-proposed groupings — each card shows its reasoning. A serotype can belong to several groups.

A · The Resistant Core

6B 9V 14 19A 19F 23F

Reasoning: these six carry the classic multidrug-resistant clones — Spain6B-2, Spain23F-1, CC230, ST320, 42% MDR in 19F. Associates 11A & 15B (penicillin non-susceptible) orbit this group.

Diagnoses: severe invasive disease, treatment-failure otitis media, meningitis (14).

B · Meningitis Watch

1 5 12F 14

Reasoning: documented meningitis links — 1 & 5 drove African meningitis-belt epidemics; 12F adult meningitis; 14 meningitis/otitis.

Diagnoses: bacterial meningitis, epidemic meningitis.

C · The Persisters

3 19A 7F

Reasoning: the ones the vaccines keep including and failing to stop — 3 sheds its capsule to dodge antibodies; 19A arrived by capsular switch after PCV7 cleared 19F; 7F shows documented vaccine failures.

Diagnoses: breakthrough invasive disease, empyema (3: 76% of childhood empyema despite vaccination).

D · Emerging IPD

8 10A 22F 33F

Reasoning: the post-PCV13 risers — 8 is 10% of adult IPD ≥65; 22F, 33F, 10A emerging invasive disease.

Diagnoses: adult invasive pneumococcal disease, pneumonia (8).

E · Ear & Carriage

6B 9V 14 19F 23F

Reasoning: the classic otitis-media and carriage serotypes — the ones children's ears knew first.

Diagnoses: otitis media, nasopharyngeal carriage.

F · The Orphans

2 20 9N 17F

Reasoning: skipped by the early conjugates — 2 & 20 live in PPSV23 only; 9N & 17F were rescued by PCV21 in 2024.

Diagnoses: adult IPD (9N), outbreak lists (2, 20).

G · Biofilm-Tested Five

3 14 18C 19F 19A

Reasoning: the exact five in the mixed S. aureus biofilm study — PCV13 serum cut their biofilms but left the staph untouched.

Diagnoses: biofilm-associated persistence; polymicrobial infection.

Not in a behavior group: serotype 4 — a PCV7-era invasive-disease serotype from before the replacement era; it sits outside the clusters above.

3 · The chemistry each group stands on

A serotype is its capsule-sugar chemistry — documented. Per-serotype sugar blueprints beyond this are open.

  • Group C's trick: serotype 3 sheds its capsule polysaccharide off the membrane, inhibiting antibody killing — chemistry as evasion.
  • 19F → 19A: a capsular switch — the MDR clone kept its resistance genes and changed its sugar coat (Taiwan19F-ST236 → 19A-ST320).
  • Conjugates: capsule sugar linked to CRM197 protein — the sugar alone (PPSV23) vs. sugar-plus-protein (PCVs) is the chemistry difference between the generations.
  • Manufacturing: every pneumococcal vaccine's polysaccharides are grown in soy-based media — soy broth upstream of every capsule in every vial.
  • 20 vs 20A: PCV21 carries 20A; cross-protection against serotype 20 is not established — one sugar tweak, open question.
