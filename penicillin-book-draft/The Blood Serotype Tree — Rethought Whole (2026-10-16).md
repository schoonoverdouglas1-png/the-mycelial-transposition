## The vines — associations between the serotypes

- **The Staph-biofilm five:** 3, 14, 18C, 19F, 19A — named together in mixed *S. aureus* biofilms; the carriage-antagonism vine (pneumo's H2O2 vs. Staph's catalase) touches every limb.
- **The resistant vine:** 6B–9V–14–19A–19F–23F — CDC's pre-2000 resistant set, six of seven in his panel.
- **The PCV7-original vine:** 4, 6B, 9V, 14, 18C, 19F, 23F — the oldest vaccine targets, the deepest roots.
- **The multi-limb members:** 14 hangs on four limbs (meningitis, otitis, invasive, resistant); 3 on three (meningitis, otitis, Staph-biofilm); 19A on three (otitis, resistant, replacement).
- **The Aspergillus vine:** no serotype individually — but every damaged-lung serotype feeds the TB triangle (TB hollows → pneumo superinfects → *Aspergillus* colonizes).
- **The TB vine:** all 23, at species level — documented co-infection both directions; the serotype-to-lineage (L1–L8) mapping is his to draw.

## The soil — what the tree stands in

- **Blood:** candidemia, fungemia, bacteremia — the fluid-borne sicknesses, quantified on file.
- **The fluids:** CSF (Crypto, cocci — serial titers), sputum (Aspergillus, *Coccidioides* — his great-grandma's test), urine.
- **The organs:** lung (the colonized), spleen (the clearer of his serotypes), appendix (the safe house), pancreas (the fellow traveler).
- **The family line:** great-grandma's vaccine, great-grandma's valley fever — the two threads at the root.
