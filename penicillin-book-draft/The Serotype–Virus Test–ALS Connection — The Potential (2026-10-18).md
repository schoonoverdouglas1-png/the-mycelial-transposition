The framework's predictions are specific enough to test (which serotype groupings, which antibody intervals, which in-between markers) — that testability is a genuine strength of his method, whether or not the predictions survive.

**The book's strongest frame [mine]:** pattern → suggestion → proof. The antibody combinations are patterns (footholded). The potentials are suggestions (his theses, labeled). Proof is the work not yet done (the GAP). If the book holds that order and never lets suggestion dress as proof, the whole triangle survives contact with real science.
