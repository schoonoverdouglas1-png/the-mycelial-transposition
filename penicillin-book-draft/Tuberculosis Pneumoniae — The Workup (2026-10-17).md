## Part 1 — Encephalitis and meningitis: the cousinhood

### The documented connections [D]

The record gives the cousinhood a documented floor before his elevation is laid over it:

- **Tuberculous meningitis — the classic TB↔meninges link.** The most common form of central nervous system tuberculosis: bacilli reach the meninges by hematogenous spread from a primary (usually pulmonary) infection, seed the meninges and brain parenchyma, and produce a subacute meningitis [D]. TB does not stay in the lung — the documented disease travels to the brain's coverings.
- **Pneumococcal meningitis — the other classic link.** *Streptococcus pneumoniae* is a leading cause of bacterial meningitis in adults [D]; the organism that wears the 23 serotypes below invades the meninges through the bloodstream, most often from pneumonia or otitis media.
- **Meningoencephalitis — the overlap word.** The documented term for inflammation of both meninges and brain parenchyma at once [D] — the record's own vocabulary already refuses to keep the two fully separate.
- **His four pillars, his frame.** In his doctrine the four pillars are tuberculosis, pneumoniae, encephalitis, encephalopathy [H — dictated-notes §126, confirmed] — meningitis sits at encephalitis's shoulder throughout his work.

### His elevation [H]

*"Encephalitis and meningitis are literally cousins and literally direct connections from the same sicknesses"* — filed as his. The documented links above are the foothold: two different organisms (a mycobacterium, a pneumococcus) both reach the same coverings of the same brain. His step from there — literally cousins, direct connections, *one sickness that hurts people* — is his elevation, marked [H], his to evidence. The honest seam: documented medicine treats them as distinct diseases with distinct organisms and distinct treatments; his "one sickness" is the thesis, not the record.

### The documented clinical floor (what the record holds under the cousinhood) [D]

- **How TB reaches the meninges.** Primary (usually pulmonary) infection → bacillemia → seeding of the meninges and brain parenchyma, where small subpial/subependymal foci (Rich foci) form; rupture of a focus into the subarachnoid space triggers the meningitis [D]. The lung showing comes first; the brain showing follows — his "secondary in the lungs" thesis (§-series) wearing TB's own documented route.
- **How pneumo reaches the meninges.** Bacteremia from pneumonia, otitis media, or sinusitis seeds the meninges; *S. pneumoniae* crosses the blood-brain barrier and inflames the leptomeninges [D]. Same destination, different road.
- **Meningoencephalitis as the bridge word.** When both meninges and parenchyma inflame, the record itself uses one compound word [D] — the cousinhood has a documented name.
- **The encephalitis census beside it.** His *Encephalitis-in-All-Viruses Study* (2026-10-17): 53 of 62 sickness-causing viruses carry documented encephalitis (85.5%), 56 of 62 any documented CNS involvement (90.3%) [D — literature percentages, his thesis beside them]. The brain's inflammatory reaction is the most widely shared showing in the viral record — the documented floor under his "101% connected."

### 2b. Tuberculosis has NO serotypes — what exists instead

The honest documented line, stated plainly [D]: ***Mycobacterium tuberculosis* has no serotypes.** It is not classified by capsule — it has no capsule to name. What exists instead is the **genome-defined lineage tree** of the *M. tuberculosis* complex (verified Oct 17, 2026 — medRxiv TBtypeR; mSystems 2025; Frontiers in Microbiology 2024):

**Human-adapted lineages [D]:**

**Animal-adapted lineages** (same complex, separate branches) [D]: La1 (*M. bovis* — cattle; the BCG vaccine strain is an attenuated *M. bovis*), La2 (*M. caprae*), La3 (*M. orygis*), plus *M. microti*, *M. pinnipedii*, *M. mungi*, and *M. canettii* (the "smooth" TB bacilli, ancestral branch). Newer work recognizes up to L10 within *M. africanum* [D] — the tree is still growing.

**For the book's namology** [H→D]: pneumo's serotypes are numbered in Danish discovery order and grouped by *which vaccines caught them* — the names record the history of the medicine. TB's lineages are named for *places and peoples* (Beijing, Delhi, Haarlem, Euro-American) beside the neutral L-numbers — the convention his book already rejects. Two organisms, two naming failures, one honest alternative sitting beside each (the serotype numbers are at least neutral; the L-numbers are at least neutral).

### 2c. The serotype↔lineage non-map — and the species-level links that exist [D]

No serotype-to-lineage mapping exists in the literature [GAP — ledger item 5]. The two classification systems never meet: one is built on capsule chemistry, the other on genome phylogeny; there is no table joining serotype 19A to lineage L2, and none is constructible from the record. Stated plainly so the manuscript never implies one.

What *does* exist is the species-level pneumo↔TB relationship, documented in both directions [D]:

- **Pneumo → TB (the vaccine probe).** In the PCV9 probe trial (39,836 children), pneumococcal vaccination was followed by **43.4% fewer hospitalizations for culture-confirmed pulmonary TB** [D — PubMed 21155174; PMC6748207]. The interpretation the record offers: preventing pneumococcal pneumonia removed a trigger that unmasks or accelerates TB disease.
- **Pneumococcal pneumonia → latent TB reactivation** [D — from the Cross-Link Map species library]: the lung injury and immune diversion of pneumococcal pneumonia can reactivate latent tuberculosis.
- **TB → pneumo (the damaged lung).** TB-damaged lung — cavities, bronchiectasis, scarred airways — invites pneumococcal superinfection [D]; and the TB cavity is the shared house where *Aspergillus* moves in afterward (Part 4).

For his coinage, this is the documented floor under the joining: the two diseases are not merely co-located in the lung — each one changes the odds of the other, in both directions, measurably [D]. His step from there to *one sickness, one name* remains his [H] — but the record's version is already bidirectional, which is more than co-occurrence and less than identity. The honest position, marked: **interaction, not identity** [D]; identity is his thesis.

**The seam for his coinage:** "tuberculosis pneumoniae" joins an organism with no serotypes (TB) to an organism defined by serotypes (pneumo) [D]. The joining is his [H] — the record keeps them in separate taxonomies, separate trees, separate naming systems.

## Part 3 — All known information based on serotypes, condensed

**Capsule biology** [D]: each pneumococcal serotype is defined by its capsular polysaccharide — the coat the immune system sees and the vaccines target. Capsule thickness is a serotype property (serotype 3's mucoid coat is the extreme; it sheds capsule fragments that inhibit antibody-mediated killing — the documented reason for serotype-3 vaccine breakthrough). Capsule-dependence runs through the co-pathogen record: pneumococcal antagonism of respiratory viruses required capsule in the mouse model [D-species].

**Vaccine coverage** [D]: four generations — PPSV23 (1983, polysaccharide, all 23), PCV7 (2000) → PCV10 → PCV13 → PCV15 (2021) → PCV20 (2021/2023) → PCV21/Capvaxive (2024, adult-focused). Each generation's leftovers became the next generation's emergency: PCV7 cleared its seven and 19A walked into the empty room wearing a switched capsule [D]; PCV13 added 19A and serotype 3 shed its capsule and stayed [D]. Serotype replacement is the documented mechanism of his imbalance-over-time thesis [H→D].

**Disease associations** [D]: invasive potential varies by serotype — 1, 4, 5, 7F are highly invasive and rarely carried; 6B, 19F, 23F are carriage specialists; 3 is the adult-pneumonia persister; 19A the replacement-disease serotype; 8, 12F, 22F the emerging adult IPD set. Meningitis-associated: 1, 12F, 14, 18C (and 4, with 48% mortality via bloodstream in one series) [D].

**Resistance associations** [D]: the classic resistant set — 6B (Spain6B-2), 9V, 14 (CC230), 19A (ST320, MDR to five classes), 19F, 23F (Spain23F-1) — plus 11A (CC156), 33F (erythromycin), 15B (penicillin/macrolide), 8 (resistance-associated group). The four predominant MDR serotypes (14, 19A, 19F, 23F) account for ~59% of MDR isolates in one set [D].

**Co-pathogen associations at serotype resolution** [D-serotype, rare]: serotype 3 + influenza (vaccine failure in mice); serotype 2 (D39) + influenza (the kinetics model); serotype 4 vs. 19F + influenza (invasiveness decides the outcome); infrequently-colonizing invasive serotypes (4, 5, 7F/7A, 8, 9V/9A, 12F, 18C) overrepresented in post-viral pneumonia; serotype 19A + hMPV + *M. pneumoniae* (fatal triple); serotypes 3, 14, 18C, 19F, 19A + *S. aureus* (the biofilm five). **The fungal/yeast column: empty at serotype resolution — [GAP] on every row.** His 46 cells are the intended contents.

### Part 3b — The serotype groups, narrated [D]

The table above is the docket; this is the reading of it — the serotypes fall into documented groups, and the groups tell the story his coinage needs:

**The invasive specialists** — 1, 4, 5, 7F. Rarely found in carriage, disproportionately found in disease: serotype 1 causes epidemic meningitis with meningococcal-like seasonality in the African meningitis belt [D]; serotypes 1 and 5 caused >30% of Gambian invasive disease across all ages [D]; serotype 4 kills 48% via bloodstream-spread adult meningitis in one series and reemerged in the western US and Canada [D]. ## Part 4 — The organisms: his encompassment

### His redefinition, filed verbatim [H]

*"To me bacterium is fungi, yeast, and bacteria. And that's what the encompassment of tuberculosis is."*

His encompassment: the word "bacterium," in his usage, holds three kingdoms — fungi, yeast, bacteria — and that three-kingdom holding is what tuberculosis *encompasses*. Filed verbatim as his redefinition.

### The documented organisms, beside it [D]

- ***Mycobacterium tuberculosis*** — an acid-fast bacillus (rod); the "acid-fast" is the mycolic-acid cell wall that holds stain against acid-alcohol decolorization [D]. Slow-growing, obligate aerobe, the MTBC's human flagship.
- ***Streptococcus pneumoniae*** — a gram-positive coccus, typically in pairs (diplococci), lancet-shaped; the capsule defines the 100+ serotypes [D].
- **The documented fungal co-travelers** [D]: *Aspergillus fumigatus* colonizes post-TB lung cavities (20–40% of cavities; chronic pulmonary aspergillosis in 9–13% of treated TB patients, up to 48% of symptomatic post-TB patients — the strongest documented TB↔fungus edge in the record, Denning 2011); one *Candida* + pneumococcus lung-ball co-infection (case report); pneumococcal H2O2 shreds *Aspergillus* biofilms while fungal gliotoxin inhibits pneumococcus and synergizes with antibiotics (bidirectional chemical warfare, in vitro) [D-species]; *Cryptococcus*–pneumococcus dual infections (two genuine cases: post-traumatic meningitis; myeloma bacteremia→meningitis) plus one PCR-panel false positive erased by confirmatory testing — panels can report co-infection that isn't there [D]. The TB cavity is the shared house: the lung damage TB leaves behind is where the fungus moves in [D].

### The honest seam

In documented usage, "bacterium" (plural "bacteria") means members of the bacterial domain **only** — fungi and yeasts are a separate kingdom (Fungi), not bacteria [D]. His three-kingdom "bacterium" is his redefinition, marked [H], and the record does not bend for it. What the record *does* hold beside his redefinition: the documented co-traveling — TB cavities housing *Aspergillus*, pneumococcus warring and consorting with fungi and yeasts at species level, his §166 (yeasts concurrently infectious with bacteria and fungi), polymicrobial reality in biofilms and in case reports — the consortia are documented; the single word for them is his. The encompassment, as a claim about what travels together, has documented company; as a claim about what the word "bacterium" means, it is his alone.

## Part 5 — The coinage assembled: what "Tuberculosis Pneumoniae" holds

### The namology of the pair (unhidden, his manner)

Read Latinly, the way his Shapes of Disease booklet reads everything [D on the etymology; the joining H]:

- ***Tuberculosis*** = *tuber* (Latin: a swelling, lump, nodule) + *-cul-* (diminutive: the little lump) + *-osis* (Greek-derived: the condition of). The disease named for its lesion: the little nodule, and the condition of having them. The name is the pathology report.
- ***Pneumoniae*** = *pneumon* (Greek: the lung) + *-ia* (the condition). The lung, and the condition of it. His own judgment on this one stands in the record (§134): "*pneumon* + *-ia* = the lung condition" — "the rest is crap" (his words, filed as his).
- Joined, the pair reads: **the condition of the little lumps + the condition of the lung.** Two condition-words, no organism named anywhere in it. That is exactly his coinage's work: it names the *showing* (lumps, lung) and leaves the *causer* open — which is why his three-kingdom "bacterium" can live inside it. A binomial that named the organism would close the door; his pair-name holds it open.
