## What "black disc" is [D]

Not a formal diagnosis — radiology shorthand. On T2 MRI, a healthy disc glows bright with water; a degenerated, desiccated disc goes dark. Black disc = the disc has dried out. The condition it points to is degenerative disc disease.

## What it's associated with [D]

Aging, mechanical wear, genetics, smoking — the documented company disc degeneration keeps. Infectious discitis (including rare fungal discitis — Candida, Aspergillus, usually post-procedure or immunocompromised) is a separate, uncommon entity.
