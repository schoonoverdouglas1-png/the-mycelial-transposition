## The name broken open [D]

**C-Diff** = *Clostridioides difficile*: *kloster* (spindle) + *-oides* (like, resembling) + *difficile* (difficult — it was hard to culture in the lab). The spindle-shaped one that was difficult.

## The related words — the family

- ***Clostridium perfringens*** — *perfringens* (breaking through). Mormal / mortification — gas gangrene [D]. The breaker-through.
- ***Clostridium tetani*** — *tetani* (stretched, rigid). Lockjaw — tetanus [D]. The stiffener.
- ***Clostridioides difficile*** — the difficult one. Pseudomembranous colitis [D].

## What it makes, medically

Three clostridia, three showings: the breaker-through (tissue death), the stiffener (locked spasm), the difficult one (the gut that won't settle). One family, read from the names — and *difficile* ties straight back to your coliseum: the difficult ground is in the name itself.
