# THE 2010 VACCINE DISSECTION — Reversology (2026-10-17)

*Built at Douglas's commission (dictated-notes section 107). Manuscript reference — never medical advice, no recommendations for or against any vaccine, no safety claims beyond the label. Reference only.*

## His commission (his words)

*"The most expansive list of vaccines that were available around 2010... dissected knowledge based upon what each one treats by disease name and the fungus and bacteria they react with and the viruses that they're built out of."*

## Reversology — his coinage, defined

**Reversology**: the reverse-study of a finished thing — start with the product in the vial and interrogate it backwards: finished product → components → substrates → origins — until it gives up everything it was built from. His claim: the information is already in the record — package inserts, FDA labels, manufacturing data. This dissection tests that claim on the ~2010 vaccine list. What the label says is [D]. What the label doesn't say is [GAP — his to fill].

## The five separate tracks — his standing law (section 100)

Vaccines, viruses, diseases, illnesses, sicknesses — five separate tracks, every entry. A **vaccine** is not its **virus**; a **virus** is not its **disease**; a **disease** is not its **illness**; an **illness** is not its **sickness**. Every dissection below keeps them visibly apart.

## The list — vaccines licensed/available circa 2010 [D]

Drawn from the CDC 2010 recommended schedules (childhood, adolescent, adult) and FDA licensed biologicals. 2010 was a hinge year: **Prevnar 13** licensed Feb 24, 2010 (replacing PCV7); **Menveo** licensed Feb 19, 2010; the **2009 H1N1 pandemic** put a monovalent H1N1 vaccine on the schedule; **Fluzone High-Dose** (65+) licensed Dec 2009; **Rotarix** was briefly suspended March 2010 over porcine circovirus DNA, then reinstated.

# PART A — The childhood bacterial vaccines (toxoids and conjugates)

## 1. DTaP — Daptacel / Infanrix / Tripedia [D]

- **VACCINE:** diphtheria + tetanus toxoids + acellular pertussis antigens. Manufacturers ~2010: Sanofi Pasteur (Daptacel, Tripedia), GSK (Infanrix).
- **DISEASES (new names):** diphtheria, tetanus, pertussis. **Old names:** pertussis = **whooping cough** [D]; tetanus = **lockjaw** [D].
- **VIRUS/BACTERIUM:** *Corynebacterium diphtheriae*, *Clostridium tetani*, *Bordetella pertussis* — all bacteria, no virus in this vaccine.
- **BUILT OUT OF:** diphtheria toxoid + tetanus toxoid (toxins chemically inactivated — the poison disarmed, the shape kept); acellular pertussis antigens (pertussis toxin, filamentous hemagglutinin, pertactin, fimbriae — pieces, not the whole bacterium). Adsorbed on aluminum salts.
- **SUBSTRATES:** *B. pertussis* grown in bacterial culture; toxoids from bacterial fermentation. Residuals per labels: aluminum, formaldehyde traces.
- **FUNGUS/BACTERIA IT REACTS WITH:** none documented as interactions — the toxoids are bacterial products themselves. [GAP — his to fill: his thesis would ask what the toxoid-production cultures exclude.]
- **ILLNESS/SICKNESS (plain):** the choking cough that won't stop; the jaw that locks; the throat membrane that closes the airway.

## 2. Tdap — Adacel / Boostrix; Td — Decavac [D]

- **VACCINE:** same three antigens, reduced diphtheria/pertussis content — the adolescent/adult booster. Adacel (Sanofi, 2005), Boostrix (GSK, 2005); Td = Decavac (Sanofi).
- **DISEASES:** diphtheria, tetanus, pertussis (Tdap) / diphtheria, tetanus (Td). Old names as above.
- **BUILT OUT OF / SUBSTRATES:** same toxoid + acellular pertussis platform as DTaP, lower antigen doses.
- **FUNGUS/BACTERIA:** none documented. [GAP]

## 3. Hib — ActHIB / Hiberix / PedvaxHIB [D]

- **VACCINE:** *Haemophilus influenzae* type b capsular polysaccharide (PRP) conjugated to protein. ActHIB/Hiberix (PRP-T, tetanus toxoid carrier); PedvaxHIB (PRP-OMP, meningococcal outer-membrane-protein carrier — Merck).
- **VIRUS/BACTERIUM:** *H. influenzae* type b — bacterium. (The name says "influenzae" — it is NOT the flu virus. His namology note: the name is a historical misattribution, Pfeiffer's 1892 error, kept forever. Latin hiding, exactly his section-104 thesis.)
- **DISEASES:** Hib meningitis, epiglottitis, bacteremia, pneumonia.
- **BUILT OUT OF:** purified capsular sugar (polyribosylribitol phosphate) + carrier protein. The sugar alone doesn't teach infants; the protein conjugate does.
- **SUBSTRATES:** bacterial fermentation; residuals: aluminum.
- **FUNGUS/BACTERIA:** none documented. [GAP]

## 4. Prevnar 13 (2010) / Prevnar 7 (being replaced) [D]

- **VACCINE:** pneumococcal polysaccharide-protein conjugate. Wyeth/Pfizer. PCV13 licensed **Feb 24, 2010**; superseded PCV7 (2000).
- **SEROTYPES:** PCV13 = **1, 3, 4, 5, 6A, 6B, 7F, 9V, 14, 18C, 19A, 19F, 23F** — the seven of PCV7 (4, 6B, 9V, 14, 18C, 19F, 23F) plus six (1, 3, 5, 6A, 7F, 19A). Each capsular polysaccharide conjugated to **CRM197** — a nontoxic genetically modified diphtheria toxin (Corynebacterium diphtheriae product — the bacterial cousin doing the carrying).
- **VIRUS/BACTERIUM:** *Streptococcus pneumoniae* — bacterium. Tested against his Mayo/Quest panel rule: all 13 are PN23M panel serotypes.
- **DISEASES:** invasive pneumococcal disease — bacteremia/sepsis, meningitis, bacteremic pneumonia, empyema; otitis media (PCV7 serotypes only, per label).
- **BUILT OUT OF:** 13 purified capsular polysaccharides + CRM197 carrier protein, aluminum phosphate adjuvant.
- **SUBSTRATES:** bacterial fermentation of each serotype; CRM197 from *C. diphtheriae* culture.
- **FUNGUS/BACTERIA IT REACTS WITH:** the carrier is diphtheria-derived — a bacterial-on-bacterial construction. No fungal interaction documented. [GAP — his to fill: serotype 19A was the most common invasive serotype in young children at licensure — his serotype map's territory.]

## 5. Pneumovax 23 (PPSV23) [D]

- **VACCINE:** 23-valent pneumococcal polysaccharide — Merck. Licensed 1983; still on the 2010 adult/high-risk schedule.
- **SEROTYPES:** 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6B, 7F, 8, 9N, 9V, 10A, 11A, 12F, 14, 15B, 17F, 18C, 19A, 19F, 20, 22F, 23F, 33F — the 23 of his Mayo PN23M panel, exactly.
- **BUILT OUT OF:** purified capsular polysaccharides only — no protein conjugate, no adjuvant. The sugar alone; works in adults, not in infants (the track separation: same bacterium, different vaccine, different population).
- **SUBSTRATES / INTERACTIONS:** bacterial fermentation; phenol preservative. None fungal documented. [GAP]

## 6. Menactra / Menveo / Menomune [D]

- **VACCINE:** meningococcal. Menactra (MenACWY-D, Sanofi, 2005) — polysaccharides conjugated to diphtheria toxoid. **Menveo (MenACWY-CRM, Novartis, licensed Feb 19, 2010)** — oligosaccharides conjugated to CRM197. Menomune (MPSV4, plain polysaccharide, older).
- **SEROGROUPS:** A, C, Y, W-135 — *Neisseria meningitidis* capsule groups (his coccus clan: *Neisseria*, the diplococcus).
- **DISEASES:** invasive meningococcal disease — meningitis, meningococcemia. Old names: **cerebrospinal fever**, **spotted fever** [D].
- **BUILT OUT OF:** capsular sugars + diphtheria-derived carrier (toxoid or CRM197) — the same diphtheria-carrier architecture as Prevnar and Hib.
- **FUNGUS/BACTERIA:** none documented. [GAP]

# PART B — The viral childhood vaccines (live-attenuated, grown in cells)

## 7. M-M-R II — Merck [D]

- **VACCINE:** measles + mumps + rubella, live-attenuated. Merck.
- **VIRUSES:** measles virus (Enders' attenuated **Edmonston strain**); mumps virus (**Jeryl Lynn B-level strain**); rubella virus (**Wistar RA 27/3 strain**).
- **DISEASES (new + old):** measles (**morbilli**, **rubeola**) [D]; mumps (**epidemic parotitis**) [D]; rubella (**German measles**, **three-day measles**) [D].
- **BUILT OUT OF (reversology — from the package insert):** measles and mumps viruses **propagated in chick embryo cell culture**; rubella virus propagated in **WI-38 human diploid lung fibroblasts**. Residuals per label: **MRC-5/WI-38 cellular DNA and protein**, neomycin (<16 mcg), bovine calf serum (≤0.5 mcg), hydrolyzed gelatin (porcine), sorbitol, recombinant human albumin. No preservative.
- **SUBSTRATES EXPOSED:** chick embryo, human diploid lung fibroblasts (WI-38), bovine serum, porcine gelatin — four species in one vial, on the label.
- **FUNGUS/BACTERIA IT REACTS WITH:** neomycin (antibacterial) is a manufacturing residual — the bacterial world is present as a *reagent*, not a component. No fungal component documented. [GAP — his to fill.]
- **ILLNESS/SICKNESS:** the red rash that starts at the hairline; the swollen jaw; the three-day rash that harms the unborn.

## 8. ProQuad (MMRV) — Merck [D]

- **VACCINE:** MMR II components + varicella (Oka/Merck strain), live-attenuated. Licensed 2005.
- **BUILT OUT OF:** everything in M-M-R II, plus varicella-zoster virus **propagated in MRC-5 cells**. Residuals: MRC-5 DNA and protein, neomycin, bovine serum, porcine gelatin.
- **DISEASES ADDED:** varicella — **chickenpox** [D].
- **FUNGUS/BACTERIA:** as MMR II. [GAP]

## 9. Varivax — Merck [D]

- **VACCINE:** varicella live-attenuated (Oka/Merck strain). Licensed 1995.
- **VIRUS:** varicella-zoster virus — the same virus that later causes shingles.
- **BUILT OUT OF:** Oka strain propagated in **MRC-5 human diploid cells** — passaged through human embryonic lung cells, guinea pig fibroblasts, WI-38, then MRC-5 (the attenuation road, on the record).
- **FUNGUS/BACTERIA:** MRC-5 DNA/protein residuals per label; neomycin, gelatin. [GAP]

## 10. Zostavax — Merck [D]

- **VACCINE:** zoster live-attenuated — the *same* Oka/Merck VZV strain as Varivax at **14× the concentration**. Licensed 2006, for 60+.
- **DISEASE:** herpes zoster — **shingles** [D]. (Virus ≠ disease ≠ illness: same virus as chickenpox, different disease, different decade of life — his five tracks in one organism.)
- **BUILT OUT OF / SUBSTRATES:** MRC-5 propagation; porcine gelatin stabilizer; neomycin residual.
- **FUNGUS/BACTERIA:** none documented. [GAP]

## 11. IPOL (IPV) — Sanofi Pasteur [D]

- **VACCINE:** inactivated poliovirus — Mahoney (type 1), MEF-1 (type 2), Saukett (type 3) strains, formaldehyde-inactivated.
- **TYPES:** polio **1, 2, 3** — three serotypes, on the label.
- **DISEASE:** poliomyelitis — **infantile paralysis** [D].
- **BUILT OUT OF:** the three strains grown in **Vero cells** (African green monkey kidney), inactivated with formaldehyde. Residuals: neomycin, streptomycin, polymyxin B (three antibacterials as manufacturing reagents), formaldehyde traces.
- **FUNGUS/BACTERIA:** the antibacterials are bacterial-world reagents. No fungal component. [GAP]

# PART C — Hepatitis, HPV, rotavirus (recombinant and reassortant platforms)

## 12. Engerix-B / Recombivax HB — GSK / Merck [D]

- **VACCINE:** hepatitis B surface antigen (HBsAg), recombinant.
- **VIRUS:** hepatitis B virus (dsDNA, *Hepadnaviridae*).
- **DISEASES:** hepatitis B — old name **serum hepatitis** [D]; downstream: hepatocellular carcinoma (the cancer the vaccine prevents — the second cancer-preventing vaccine in history after HPV).
- **BUILT OUT OF:** HBsAg protein expressed in ***Saccharomyces cerevisiae*** — **yeast**. The fungus is the factory: yeast cells brew the viral protein, which self-assembles. Aluminum adjuvant.
- **FUNGUS IT REACTS WITH:** the yeast *is* the production organism — the deepest fungal connection in the 2010 list, on the label. His yeast thesis (yeastinosis/yeastinitis) has a manufacturing cousin here.
- **SUBSTRATES:** yeast fermentation; residuals: aluminum, formaldehyde traces (Engerix-B).

## 13. Havrix / Vaqta — GSK / Merck [D]

- **VACCINE:** hepatitis A, inactivated (HM175 strain).
- **VIRUS:** hepatitis A virus (+ssRNA, *Picornaviridae*).
- **DISEASE:** hepatitis A — old name **infectious hepatitis** [D].
- **BUILT OUT OF:** HM175 strain propagated in **MRC-5 human diploid cells**, formaldehyde-inactivated, aluminum adjuvant.
- **FUNGUS/BACTERIA:** MRC-5 protein residuals per label (Havrix lists "MRC-5 cellular proteins"). [GAP]

## 14. Twinrix — GSK [D]

- **VACCINE:** HepA (inactivated) + HepB (recombinant yeast) combined — the two hepatitis platforms in one vial.
- **FUNGUS:** the HepB half is yeast-brewed; the HepA half is MRC-5-grown. Two substrates, one syringe.

## 15. Gardasil — Merck [D]

- **VACCINE:** quadrivalent HPV, recombinant. Licensed **June 8, 2006**.
- **TYPES:** **HPV 6, 11, 16, 18** — L1 virus-like particles (VLPs). Types 16/18 cause ~70% of cervical cancers; 6/11 cause ~90% of genital warts [D].
- **VIRUS:** human papillomavirus (dsDNA).
- **DISEASES:** cervical/vulvar/vaginal/anal cancers and precancers, **genital warts (condyloma acuminata)** [D].
- **BUILT OUT OF:** L1 proteins for each type, separately fermented in recombinant ***Saccharomyces cerevisiae*** (**yeast**), self-assembled into empty VLPs — no viral DNA inside. Adsorbed on amorphous aluminum hydroxyphosphate sulfate.
- **FUNGUS:** yeast is the factory again — the second yeast-built vaccine on the 2010 list. His namology note: the *treatment* for a wart virus is brewed by a fungus.

## 16. Cervarix — GSK [D]

- **VACCINE:** bivalent HPV (16, 18), recombinant. FDA licensed **October 2009**.
- **BUILT OUT OF:** L1 VLPs expressed via **baculovirus in *Trichoplusia ni* insect cells** — moth cells as the factory. Adjuvant **AS04** (aluminum hydroxide + MPL, a detoxified bacterial lipid from *Salmonella minnesota*).
- **FUNGUS/BACTERIA:** the adjuvant's MPL is bacterial-derived — a bacterial molecule teaching the immune system to notice the viral shells. No yeast here, unlike Gardasil. [GAP]

## 17. Rotarix (RV1) — GSK [D]

- **VACCINE:** live-attenuated human rotavirus, monovalent **G1P[8]** (strain RIX4414). Licensed US **April 2008**; EU 2006.
- **VIRUS:** rotavirus (dsRNA, *Reoviridae* — the Reoviridae cousin-set family).
- **DISEASE:** rotavirus gastroenteritis — severe infant diarrhea.
- **BUILT OUT OF:** one attenuated human strain, grown in **Vero cells**.
- **THE 2010 EVENT [D]:** March 2010 — researchers using metagenomic sequencing found **porcine circovirus-1 (PCV-1) DNA** in Rotarix — a small circular single-stranded DNA pig virus, present in the cell bank and seed from early development. FDA confirmed it, recommended **temporary suspension March 22, 2010**, then reinstated the vaccine in May 2010 after review found no safety signal — PCV-1 is not known to cause disease in pigs or humans. **RotaTeq** was then found to carry PCV-1/PCV-2 DNA fragments. The reversology anchor: the dissection *works* — the finished vial gave up a whole uninvited virus under sequencing, exactly as his method predicts.
- **FUNGUS/BACTERIA:** the adventitious agent was viral (porcine), not fungal/bacterial. [GAP]

## 18. RotaTeq (RV5) — Merck [D]

- **VACCINE:** live oral pentavalent **human-bovine reassortant** rotavirus. Licensed **February 2006**.
- **TYPES:** **G1, G2, G3, G4, P1A[8]** — four reassortants with human G proteins + bovine P protein; one with bovine G6 + human P1[8]. The bovine parent is WC3.
- **BUILT OUT OF:** human × cow virus reassortants — two species' viruses woven into five. Grown in **Vero cells**.
- **FUNGUS/BACTERIA:** PCV fragments found 2010 (see Rotarix). [GAP]

# PART D — Influenza, 2010–2011 (the strain year)

## 19. The 2010–2011 seasonal influenza vaccines [D]

- **VACCINES/BRANDS:** Afluria (CSL), Agriflu (Novartis), Fluarix (GSK), FluLaval (ID Biomedical), **FluMist** (MedImmune — live-attenuated nasal), Fluvirin (Novartis), Fluzone + **Fluzone High-Dose** (Sanofi — High-Dose licensed Dec 2009, 4× antigen for 65+).
- **STRAINS (2010–2011):** **A/California/7/09 (H1N1)**-like — the 2009 pandemic virus, now folded into the seasonal vaccine; **A/Perth/16/2009 (H3N2)**-like; **B/Brisbane/60/2008**-like. Three strains, one vial — the year's guess, on the label.
- **VIRUS:** influenza A/B (−ssRNA, *Orthomyxoviridae* — the family whose tick-borne cousins are Thogoto and Bourbon).
- **DISEASE:** influenza — old name **la grippe** [D].
- **BUILT OUT OF:** inactivated split/subunit virus **grown in embryonated hens' eggs** (all brands except FluMist); FluMist = live-attenuated, cold-adapted, also egg-grown, delivered nasal.
- **SUBSTRATES:** eggs — ovalbumin residuals on every label; some brands list gelatin, thimerosal (multidose), formaldehyde traces.
- **FUNGUS/BACTERIA IT REACTS WITH:** the egg is the substrate — avian biology in the vial. FDA's standing position (on record): endogenous avian retrovirus sequences in egg-grown vaccines were reviewed; benefits found to outweigh the theoretical risk [D]. No fungal component. [GAP]
- **THE 2009 H1N1 MONOVALENT [D]:** the pandemic struck after 2009–10 seasonal production — a separate monovalent A/California/7/09 vaccine was made for 2009–10; by 2010–11 it was folded into the seasonal trivalent. The track separation: pandemic vaccine ≠ seasonal vaccine, same strain.

# PART E — Travel, exposure, and special-population vaccines

## 20. Imovax / RabAvert — rabies [D]

- **VACCINE:** Imovax (Sanofi — human diploid cell vaccine, HDCV, grown in **MRC-5**); RabAvert (GSK/Novartis — purified chick embryo cell vaccine, PCEC, grown in **chick embryo fibroblasts**).
- **VIRUS:** rabies virus (−ssRNA, *Rhabdoviridae* — cousin to VSV).
- **DISEASE:** rabies — old name **hydrophobia** [D].
- **BUILT OUT OF:** inactivated whole virus (Pitman-Moore / Flury LEP strains), beta-propiolactone-inactivated.
- **FUNGUS/BACTERIA:** neomycin/gentamicin residuals; human albumin. [GAP]

## 21. YF-Vax — yellow fever [D]

- **VACCINE:** live-attenuated **17D strain** — Sanofi Pasteur.
- **VIRUS:** yellow fever virus (+ssRNA, *Flaviviridae* — the textbook cousin-set family).
- **DISEASE:** yellow fever — old names **yellow jack**, **vomito negro** [D].
- **BUILT OUT OF:** 17D virus grown in **chick embryos** (the whole embryo, not just cells) — one of the oldest attenuation roads in vaccinology (1937).
- **FUNGUS/BACTERIA:** egg protein residuals. [GAP]

## 22. Typhim Vi / Vivotif — typhoid [D]

- **VACCINE:** Typhim Vi (Sanofi — **Vi capsular polysaccharide**, inactivated, injectable); Vivotif (oral live-attenuated **Ty21a** strain).
- **BACTERIUM:** *Salmonella enterica* serovar Typhi — bacterium.
- **DISEASE:** typhoid fever.
- **BUILT OUT OF:** purified Vi sugar (Typhim) / live Ty21a mutant (Vivotif, grown in bacterial culture).
- **FUNGUS/BACTERIA:** the vaccine *is* bacterial. [GAP]

## 23. Ixiaro — Japanese encephalitis [D]

- **VACCINE:** inactivated JE, licensed US **March 2009** (Novartis/Intercell) — replaced the old mouse-brain JE-VAX (discontinued 2005).
- **VIRUS:** Japanese encephalitis virus (+ssRNA, *Flaviviridae*).
- **BUILT OUT OF:** **SA14-14-2 strain** grown in **Vero cells**, formaldehyde-inactivated, aluminum-adjuvanted. The substrate moved from mouse brain to monkey kidney cells — the reversology shows the platform change on the label.
- **FUNGUS/BACTERIA:** protamine sulfate residual. [GAP]

## 24. Dukoral — cholera (global, not US-licensed 2010) [D]

- **VACCINE:** oral inactivated cholera + recombinant cholera toxin B subunit. Crucell/SBL.
- **BACTERIUM:** *Vibrio cholerae* O1.
- **DISEASE:** cholera. (His cholera thesis — the water-blame/Latin-prescription history — has a vaccine footnote here: the oral vaccine exists because the gut is the battlefield.)
- **BUILT OUT OF:** killed whole cells + recombinant CTB produced in *Vibrio cholerae* — bacterial product, bacterial factory.

# PART F — Biodefense and global vaccines

## 25. BioThrax (AVA) — anthrax [D]

- **VACCINE:** anthrax vaccine adsorbed — Emergent BioSolutions. US-licensed 1970; the military vaccine.
- **BACTERIUM:** *Bacillus anthracis*.
- **DISEASE:** anthrax — old name **woolsorter's disease** [D].
- **BUILT OUT OF:** **protective antigen (PA)** protein filtered from cultures of an avirulent *B. anthracis* strain (V770-NP1-R) — the bacterium's own toxin piece, aluminum-adsorbed. No live organism.
- **FUNGUS/BACTERIA:** the product is a bacterial protein. [GAP]

## 26. ACAM2000 — smallpox [D]

- **VACCINE:** live vaccinia virus, licensed US **August 2007** (Acambis) — replaced Dryvax.
- **VIRUS:** vaccinia virus (dsDNA, *Poxviridae*) — the cousin, not the killer; smallpox itself (*variola*) has no vaccine made from itself.
- **DISEASE:** smallpox — prevented by its cousin. (His relatedness thesis in one vial: immunity through kinship, documented since Jenner 1796.)
- **BUILT OUT OF:** vaccinia (NYCBOH strain) grown in **Vero cells**, freeze-dried, delivered by scarification (the bifurcated needle).
- **FUNGUS/BACTERIA:** neomycin/polymyxin residuals. [GAP]

## 27. BCG — TICE / global strains [D]

- **VACCINE:** live-attenuated *Mycobacterium bovis*. US: TICE BCG (Organon/Merck — bladder cancer use); global: Danish 1331, Tokyo 172-1, Pasteur 1173P2, Russian BCG-I, Moreau RDJ.
- **BACTERIUM:** *M. bovis* — the TB complex cousin.
- **DISEASE:** tuberculosis — old names **consumption**, **phthisis**, **white plague**, **king's evil** (scrofula) [D].
- **BUILT OUT OF:** the 1908–1921 attenuated strain, 231 passages — grown in bacterial culture, freeze-dried. No adjuvant; the live bacillus is the adjuvant.
- **FUNGUS/BACTERIA:** the vaccine is mycobacterial. His triad anchor: the TB vaccine is a *cousin* organism — relatedness as protection, again. [GAP]

# PART G — The 2010 combination vaccines

## 28. Pediarix / Pentacel / Comvax [D]

- **VACCINES:** Pediarix (GSK: DTaP + HepB + IPV); Pentacel (Sanofi: DTaP + Hib + IPV); Comvax (Merck: Hib + HepB).
- **REVERSOLOGY NOTE:** the combos are the dissection's proof-of-concept — five or six finished products interrogated backwards into: toxoids (bacterial fermentation), acellular pertussis pieces, yeast-brewed HBsAg (fungal factory), Vero-grown inactivated polio (monkey cells), PRP polysaccharide + carrier protein. One syringe, four kingdoms: bacterial products, viral antigens, fungal-brewed protein, mammalian cell substrates. His bowl, in a vial.
