## The five separate tracks — his standing law (section 100)

His rule, stated up front because it governs every entry below: **vaccines, viruses, diseases, illnesses, and sicknesses are FIVE SEPARATE TRACKS**, thought about separately every time medicine is discussed:

- a **VIRUS** is not its disease,
- a **DISEASE** is not its illness,
- an **ILLNESS** is not its sickness,
- a **VACCINE** is not any of them.

His namology already held illness/sickness/disease as three different places (Oct 15/16): **disease** = the named medical entity (what the textbooks call it); **illness** = what the person lives through and feels (the lived experience); **sickness** = the old word for the state of being sick (the plain human word). This file adds **virus** (the organism, the rider) and **vaccine** (the rehearsal, the mimicry — his section-90 principle made into a product) as two more tracks. Every entry below keeps all five visibly separate. Never collapse them into one line.

## How to read this expansion

Organized **by virus family**. For each major virus, nine blocks, each its own track:

1. **VIRUS** — genome type, ride (transmission), host.
2. **DISEASE** — diseases by name, new names [D].
3. **ILLNESS · SICKNESS** — the lived experience and the old plain word, in his language.
4. **OLD NAMES** — verified old names only [D]; [GAP] where none is documented.
5. **LABORATORY** — everything lab-wise: serology (IgM/IgG), molecular (PCR/NAAT), antigen, culture, named panels.
6. **FUNGAL element** — where the virus's story touches fungi (galactomannan, beta-D-glucan, fungal culture where they belong).
7. **BACTERIAL element** — where it touches bacteria (cultures, Gram stain, superinfection workups).
8. **PESTILENCE element** — "pestilism" (his section-64 coinage): epidemic/plague history, pandemic potential, notifiable status [D].
9. **VACCINE** — licensed products (name, type, target) with sources; **pipeline kept in a separate marked subsection, never mixed**; "no licensed vaccine" stated plainly where true — that absence is itself a documented finding, not a gap.

Tier tags: **[D]** documented · **[H]** his · **[H→D]** his with documented foothold · **[GAP — his to fill]** the record ends here. His 88% bar holds: nothing below it unless marked gap. Minor viruses in each family are compressed to table rows carrying the same nine tracks in brief.

Cross-links point backward to the Full Tree (FT), the triad tree (TT), the serotype map (SM), and the virology list (VL).

# PART A — Families WITH arboviral cousins (full detail)

## 1. Flaviviridae (+ssRNA) — the textbook cousin set

*Kinship: single-ORF polyprotein, envelope E protein with the conserved fusion loop — the record's VNA for this family [D]. The ride varies (mosquito, tick, blood); the kinship doesn't.*

### Dengue virus 1–4

- **VIRUS:** +ssRNA flavivirus; ride: *Aedes* mosquito (day-biter); host: humans (urban cycle), primates. Four serotypes — infection with one does not protect against the others [D].
- **DISEASE:** dengue fever; dengue hemorrhagic fever; dengue shock syndrome [D].
- **ILLNESS · SICKNESS:** the sick feeling is sudden high fever with crushing bone-and-joint pain — the old hands called the sickness by what it felt like (see old names).
- **OLD NAMES:** **"breakbone fever"** — exact old name, named for the pain [D].
- **LABORATORY:** serology — IgM (acute, appears ~day 4–5), IgG (past infection; **cross-reacts with Zika** — the cousin problem, his thesis in the lab) [D]; antigen — **NS1** (acute-phase, days 1–5) [D]; molecular — RT-PCR (acute viremia, also serotypes 1–4) [D]; confirmatory — PRNT where cross-reaction confuses the picture [D].
- **FUNGAL:** [GAP — no documented dengue↔fungus link at 88%].
- **BACTERIAL:** severe dengue workups include blood cultures — not because dengue is bacterial, but to rule out bacterial sepsis wearing dengue's clothes [D].
- **PESTILENCE:** ~390 million infections yearly, range expanding with the mosquito [D]; the archetypal urban mosquito plague.
- **VACCINE (licensed):** **Qdenga (TAK-003)** — live-attenuated tetravalent, DENV-2 backbone carrying DENV-1/3/4 surface proteins; licensed in 41 countries (EU authorized Dec 2022, ages 4+), WHO-prequalified; 7-year trial: 61.2% efficacy against confirmed dengue, 84.1% against hospitalization [D]. **Dengvaxia** — chimeric live-attenuated tetravalent; **EU marketing authorization WITHDRAWN October 2025**; was restricted to 6–45-year-olds with test-confirmed prior dengue [D]. United States has licensed neither.
- **VACCINE (pipeline, separate):** Merck V181 (Phase 3); others in Phase 1–2 [D].

### Zika virus

- **VIRUS:** +ssRNA flavivirus; ride: *Aedes* mosquito; also sexual/vertical transmission [D].
- **DISEASE:** Zika fever; congenital Zika syndrome (microcephaly) [D].
- **ILLNESS · SICKNESS:** usually a mild sickness — fever, rash, joint pain; the devastation is in the unborn, not the bitten.
- **OLD NAMES:** none documented (described 1947, Uganda) [D].
- **LABORATORY:** molecular — RT-PCR of serum/urine (acute, urine sheds longer) [D]; serology — IgM, but **cross-reacts with dengue** → PRNT confirmatory required [D].
- **FUNGAL:** [GAP]. **BACTERIAL:** [GAP].
- **PESTILENCE:** 2015–2016 Americas epidemic; WHO public-health emergency [D].
- **VACCINE (licensed):** none licensed [D] — stated plainly.
- **VACCINE (pipeline, separate):** mRNA candidates in clinical trials [D].

### Yellow fever virus

- **VIRUS:** +ssRNA flavivirus; ride: mosquito (*Aedes/Haemagogus*); the family's namesake [D].
- **DISEASE:** yellow fever [D].
- **ILLNESS · SICKNESS:** fever, then the yellowing — jaundice — then the black vomit in the worst of it.
- **OLD NAMES:** **"yellow jack"** · **"vomito negro"** (black vomit) [D].
- **LABORATORY:** serology — IgM (acute); molecular — RT-PCR (acute viremia) [D]; PRNT for confirmation [D].
- **FUNGAL:** [GAP]. **BACTERIAL:** [GAP].
- **PESTILENCE:** the great historic port plague — 1793 Philadelphia, the Panama Canal's killer; still notifiable, still killing in Africa and South America [D].
- **VACCINE (licensed):** **YF-Vax / Stamaril** — live-attenuated 17D strain, single dose, among the most effective vaccines ever made (decades of protection) [D].

### West Nile virus

- **VIRUS:** +ssRNA flavivirus; ride: mosquito (*Culex*), birds the reservoir [D].
- **DISEASE:** West Nile fever; West Nile neuroinvasive disease (encephalitis, meningitis, flaccid paralysis) [D].
- **ILLNESS · SICKNESS:** most feel nothing; the few feel the brain swelling.
- **OLD NAMES:** none documented [D].
- **LABORATORY:** serology — **IgM in serum or CSF** (the neuroinvasive workup) [D]; confirmatory — PRNT (St. Louis encephalitis cross-reacts) [D]; molecular — RT-PCR [D].
- **FUNGAL:** [GAP]. **BACTERIAL:** CSF workup includes bacterial cultures — meningitis must be sorted bacterial-vs-viral [D].
- **PESTILENCE:** swept North America from 1999; now endemic, seasonal [D].
- **VACCINE (licensed):** none licensed for humans [D] — stated plainly (veterinary equine vaccines exist, a different track).

### Japanese encephalitis virus · St. Louis encephalitis virus · Tick-borne encephalitis virus · Powassan virus

| Virus | Disease (new) | Old names | Laboratory | Vaccine (licensed) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| JEV (mosquito) | Japanese encephalitis [D] | none documented [D] | IgM serum/CSF; RT-PCR [D] | **Ixiaro** — inactivated, Vero-cell [D] |
| SLEV (mosquito) | St. Louis encephalitis [D] | none documented [D] | IgM + PRNT (WNV cross-reacts) [D] | none licensed [D] |
| TBEV (tick) | tick-borne encephalitis [D] | "Central European encephalitis" (old regional) [D] | IgM/IgG serology; RT-PCR [D] | **FSME-Immun / Encepur** — inactivated (EU) [D] |
| Powassan (tick) | Powassan encephalitis [D] | none documented [D] | serology + PRNT [D] | none licensed [D] |

- **FUNGAL / BACTERIAL** for all four: [GAP] at 88% — encephalitis workups sort bacterial vs viral by CSF culture and Gram stain, but no documented fungus/bacterium *companionship* with these viruses.
- **PESTILENCE:** JEV — the leading vaccine-preventable encephalitis of Asia [D]; TBEV — expanding European range [D].

### Hepatitis C virus — the non-arboviral cousin (*Hepacivirus*)

- **VIRUS:** +ssRNA flavivirus-family; ride: blood — needles, transfusion (pre-screening era), birth [D]; NOT arthropod — same kinship, different ride [D].
- **DISEASE:** hepatitis C; chronic hepatitis; cirrhosis; hepatocellular carcinoma [D].
- **ILLNESS · SICKNESS:** the quiet sickness — decades silent, then the liver fails.
- **OLD NAMES:** **"non-A, non-B hepatitis"** — exact old name, pre-1989 [D].
- **LABORATORY:** serology — **anti-HCV antibody** (screen) [D]; molecular — **HCV RNA PCR** (confirms active infection) + **genotype testing** (1–6, guides the old regimens) [D]; the classic two-step: antibody says *exposed*, RNA says *infected now* [D].
- **FUNGAL:** [GAP]. **BACTERIAL:** [GAP].
- **PESTILENCE:** ~50 million chronic infections worldwide; the transfusion plague of the late 20th century [D].
- **VACCINE (licensed):** none licensed [D] — stated plainly; on WHO's urgent vaccine-development list [D].
- **VACCINE (pipeline, separate):** candidates in Phase 1–2; no Phase 3 success to date [D].

**Family cross-links:** FT Part A (flavivirus chains); VL Test 1 (dengue↔Zika cross-reactivity, ADE); his panels touch none of these directly — the flavivirus lesson for his book is the *cousin* lesson: relatedness → cross-reaction → sometimes protection, sometimes enhancement.

## 2. Togaviridae (+ssRNA)

*Kinship: one genus rides insects (*Alphavirus*), the other rides air (*Rubivirus* — rubella alone). The ride diverges; the kinship holds [H→D].*

### Chikungunya virus

- **VIRUS:** +ssRNA alphavirus; ride: *Aedes* mosquito [D].
- **DISEASE:** chikungunya fever; chronic inflammatory arthralgia (months–years) [D].
- **ILLNESS · SICKNESS:** sudden fever with joint pain so severe it bends people double.
- **OLD NAMES:** "chikungunya" itself — Makonde for **"that which bends up"**; the name *is* the showing [D].
- **LABORATORY:** molecular — RT-PCR (acute, first week) [D]; serology — IgM (acute/convalescent), IgG (past) [D].
- **FUNGAL:** [GAP]. **BACTERIAL:** [GAP].
- **PESTILENCE:** 2005–2006 Réunion outbreak; now in 110+ countries; 43% suffer chronic joint disease [D].
- **VACCINE (licensed):** **Ixchiq (Valneva)** — single-dose live-attenuated; FDA accelerated approval **November 2023** — then the honest turn: the FDA **SUSPENDED its license on August 22, 2025** after 20+ chikungunya-like illnesses in recipients, 21 hospitalizations, and 3 deaths including one encephalitis with vaccine-strain virus in CSF; confirmatory efficacy studies never completed [D]. Licensed-then-suspended: the record, stated whole.
- **VACCINE (pipeline, separate):** other candidates (virus-like particle, mRNA) in trials [D].

### Eastern / Western / Venezuelan equine encephalitis viruses

- **VIRUS:** +ssRNA alphaviruses; ride: mosquito; hosts: birds/rodents → horses → humans [D].
- **DISEASE:** EEE / WEE / VEE encephalitis [D].
- **ILLNESS · SICKNESS:** the brain-fever sickness; EEE kills roughly a third of the symptomatic.
- **OLD NAMES:** none in common use beyond the geographic tags [D].
- **LABORATORY:** serology — IgM + PRNT confirmatory [D]; molecular — RT-PCR [D].
- **FUNGAL / BACTERIAL:** [GAP].
- **PESTILENCE:** EEE — the deadliest endemic mosquito encephalitis in North America; VEE — epizootics sweeping horse populations [D]; all three are select agents (U.S.) [D].
- **VACCINE (licensed):** none licensed for humans [D] — stated plainly (inactivated equine vaccines exist for horses — a different track; investigational human vaccines under IND only).

### Rubella virus — the non-arboviral cousin (*Rubivirus*)

- **VIRUS:** +ssRNA; ride: respiratory droplets [D]; the family's only non-insect genus, rubella its sole member [D].
- **DISEASE:** rubella; congenital rubella syndrome [D].
- **ILLNESS · SICKNESS:** a mild rash-sickness in children; a catastrophe in the womb.
- **OLD NAMES:** **"German measles"** — geographic; German physicians first distinguished it from measles [D].
- **LABORATORY:** serology — **IgM (acute)** / **IgG (immunity screening — the prenatal panel)** [D]; molecular — RT-PCR [D].
- **FUNGAL / BACTERIAL:** [GAP].
- **PESTILENCE:** the great teratogenic plague — 1964–65 U.S. epidemic caused ~20,000 congenital cases; vaccine-era near-elimination [D].
- **VACCINE (licensed):** **MMR** — live-attenuated measles-mumps-rubella (also MMRV with varicella); the rubella component is the reason congenital rubella nearly vanished where used [D].

**Family cross-links:** FT Part A (togavirus chains); the chikungunya vaccine story belongs in his book's honesty chapter — approved on antibody titers, suspended on safety: the accelerated-approval arc, fully documented.

## 3. Bunyavirales, order (−ssRNA, segmented) — his panel's order

*Kinship: three genome segments (small/medium/large — nucleocapsid, glycoproteins, polymerase). His arbopanel viruses live here [D].*

### La Crosse virus ⭐ — HIS panel virus

- **VIRUS:** −ssRNA orthobunyavirus, California serogroup; ride: mosquito (*Aedes triseriatus*, tree-hole) [D].
- **DISEASE:** La Crosse encephalitis [D].
- **ILLNESS · SICKNESS:** the child's brain-fever — mostly mild, sometimes seizures.
- **OLD NAMES:** none separate — it *is* the modern name of what was once just called "California encephalitis" in the region [D].
- **LABORATORY:** serology — **IgM**, then **PRNT confirmatory** — because California serogroup cousins cross-react (up to 23% cross-reactivity with Jamestown Canyon in JCV patients) [D]; molecular — RT-PCR [D]. **His panel lives inside this cross-reactivity: the test needs confirmation *because* the cousins are kin — his thesis, documented, inside his own test.**
- **FUNGAL:** [GAP]. **BACTERIAL:** encephalitis workups include CSF bacterial culture and Gram stain to sort bacterial from viral [D].
- **PESTILENCE:** the leading endemic pediatric arboviral encephalitis in the U.S. [D].
- **VACCINE (licensed):** none licensed [D] — stated plainly.

### Jamestown Canyon virus ⭐ — HIS panel virus

- **VIRUS:** −ssRNA orthobunyavirus, California serogroup; ride: many mosquito species — no strict vector range [D]; first isolated 1961, Jamestown Canyon, Colorado [D].
- **DISEASE:** Jamestown Canyon encephalitis [D].
- **ILLNESS · SICKNESS:** adult brain-fever, often under-recognized.
- **OLD NAMES:** none separate [D].
- **LABORATORY:** serology — IgM + **PRNT** (the La Crosse cross-reactivity runs both ways) [D]; molecular — RT-PCR [D].
- **FUNGAL:** [GAP]. **BACTERIAL:** [GAP].
- **PESTILENCE:** likely under-diagnosed; rising recognition [D].
- **VACCINE (licensed):** none licensed [D].

### Rift Valley fever virus · Crimean-Congo hemorrhagic fever virus · Oropouche virus · Hantaviruses

| Virus | Disease (new) | Old names | Laboratory | Vaccine (licensed) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| RVFV (mosquito, *Phlebovirus*) | Rift Valley fever [D] | none documented [D] | IgM/IgG serology; RT-PCR [D] | none for humans (veterinary Smithburn live vaccine exists — different track) [D] |
| CCHFV (tick, *Orthonairovirus*) | Crimean-Congo hemorrhagic fever [D] | "Congo fever" (old regional) [D] | RT-PCR (acute); serology [D] | none licensed [D] — pipeline candidates [D] |
| Oropouche (midge, *Culicoides*) | Oropouche fever [D] | none documented [D] | RT-PCR; serology [D] | none licensed [D] |

- **FUNGAL / BACTERIAL** for all: [GAP] at 88%.
- **PESTILENCE:** CCHF — notifiable, high case-fatality, the tick-borne hemorrhagic fever of record [D]; RVF — livestock epizootics with human spillover [D]; Sin Nombre — the 1993 Four Corners outbreak that named a new disease [D].

## 4. Reoviridae (dsRNA, segmented)

### Colorado tick fever virus

- **VIRUS:** dsRNA coltivirus — **12 segments, ~29,000 bp**, among the largest arbovirus genomes [D]; ride: tick (*Dermacentor andersoni*) [D].
- **DISEASE:** Colorado tick fever [D].
- **ILLNESS · SICKNESS:** the saddleback fever — fever that leaves and returns.
- **OLD NAMES:** **"mountain tick fever"** (old regional) [D].
- **LABORATORY:** molecular — RT-PCR [D]; serology — historically complement fixation, now immunofluorescence [D].
- **FUNGAL / BACTERIAL:** [GAP].
- **PESTILENCE:** regional, not pandemic; the Rocky Mountain tick's signature [D].
- **VACCINE (licensed):** none licensed [D].

### Rotavirus — the non-arboviral cousin (*Sedoreovirinae*)

- **VIRUS:** dsRNA; ride: fecal–oral [D]; same segmented kinship, rides dirty water instead of a tick [D].
- **DISEASE:** rotavirus gastroenteritis [D].
- **ILLNESS · SICKNESS:** the dehydrating diarrhea of childhood — "stomach flu" in the folk mouth (no flu virus involved — his namology file notes this borrowing [D]).
- **OLD NAMES:** none separate [D].
- **LABORATORY:** **stool antigen EIA** (the workhorse) [D]; molecular — RT-PCR [D].
- **FUNGAL / BACTERIAL:** stool workups include bacterial culture (*Salmonella*, *Shigella*, *Campylobacter*) to sort the cause [D].
- **PESTILENCE:** the great child-killer of the pre-vaccine era — hundreds of thousands of deaths yearly [D].
- **VACCINE (licensed):** **RotaTeq** (live pentavalent) and **Rotarix** (live monovalent) — among the most successful pediatric vaccines in history [D].

## 5. Rhabdoviridae (−ssRNA)

*The family that holds the most feared non-vector virus and genuine insect-borne members side by side — kinship ignoring the ride [H→D].*

### Rabies virus — the non-arboviral cousin (*Lyssavirus*)

- **VIRUS:** −ssRNA; ride: animal bite/saliva — NOT arthropod [D].
- **DISEASE:** rabies [D].
- **ILLNESS · SICKNESS:** the horror-sickness — agitation, then the throat closing on water.

- **PESTILENCE:** near-100% fatal once symptomatic — the oldest terror in the book; still kills tens of thousands yearly where vaccine is scarce [D].
- **VACCINE (licensed):** **RabAvert / Imovax** — inactivated cell-culture vaccines; pre-exposure and the famous post-exposure series (vaccine + immunoglobulin) [D].

### Vesicular stomatitis virus · Chandipura virus (arthropod-borne cousins, *Vesiculovirus*)

- **DISEASE:** vesicular stomatitis (livestock; mild human); Chandipura encephalitis (India) [D].
- **OLD NAMES:** none documented [D].
- **LABORATORY:** serology; RT-PCR [D].
- **FUNGAL / BACTERIAL:** [GAP].
- **PESTILENCE:** livestock plague; Chandipura — pediatric encephalitis outbreaks [D].
- **VACCINE (licensed):** none for humans [D] — noted: the VSV *platform* carries the Ebola vaccine (Ervebo is a VSV with Ebola's coat) — the cousin carrying the cure [D].

## 6. Orthomyxoviridae (−ssRNA, segmented) — the flu's family

*The polymerase shares the deeply conserved ANP32 host-factor dependence across a billion years of host divergence [D] — kinship at the deepest level.*

### Influenza A, B (C in humans)

- **VIRUS:** −ssRNA, 8 segments; ride: respiratory droplets [D].
- **DISEASE:** influenza; viral pneumonia; secondary bacterial pneumonia [D].
- **ILLNESS · SICKNESS:** the knock-flat sickness — fever, muscle ache, the bed for a week.
- **OLD NAMES:** **"la grippe"** (French, "the seizure") [D]; **"Spanish flu"** (1918 — the misnomer that stuck; it wasn't Spanish) [D].
- **LABORATORY:** molecular — **RT-PCR** (the gold standard) [D]; antigen — rapid influenza tests (fast, less sensitive) [D]; culture — viral culture (surveillance, slow) [D]; serology — hemagglutination inhibition (research/surveillance, not diagnosis) [D]; **named panels — multiplex respiratory viral panels** (flu A/B + RSV + rhinovirus + others on one run) [D].
- **FUNGAL:** **influenza-associated aspergillosis** — documented in ICU influenza pneumonia; the flu opens the door and *Aspergillus* walks in — galactomannan and fungal culture enter the workup [D]. *(Cross-link: TT Edge 3, the pneumo↔Aspergillus chemical warfare.)*
- **BACTERIAL:** the classic superinfection — **flu → pneumococcal pneumonia** (serotypes 2/3/4/19F in the models [D-serotype, SM]), **flu → *S. aureus* pneumonia**; the workup is sputum culture, blood cultures, Gram stain [D]. *(Cross-link: SM directionality table.)*
- **PESTILENCE:** 1918 (50M+ dead), 1957, 1968, 2009 — the pandemic family [D].
- **VACCINE (licensed):** **IIV** (inactivated, the yearly shot), **LAIV** (live-attenuated nasal spray), **Flublok** (recombinant) — reformulated yearly against predicted strains [D].
- **VACCINE (pipeline, separate):** mRNA-1010 (Phase 3); universal-flu candidates [D].

### Thogoto · Dhori · Bourbon viruses — the tick-borne cousins (*Thogotovirus*)

- **VIRUS:** −ssRNA segmented; ride: ticks [D]; the flu's own cousins ride ticks [D].
- **DISEASE:** Thogoto febrile illness (neuromyelitis optica, Nigeria 1966); Bourbon virus disease (fatal Kansas case, 2014) [D].
- **OLD NAMES:** none (Bourbon named for Bourbon County, Kansas — geographic, 2014) [D].
- **LABORATORY:** serology; RT-PCR [D].
- **FUNGAL / BACTERIAL:** [GAP].
- **PESTILENCE:** emerging; ~1% seropositivity in endemic Missouri for Bourbon [D].
- **VACCINE (licensed):** none [D].

**Family cross-links:** FT Part A (orthomyxovirus chains); SM (flu→pneumo serotype cells); TT (the triad's viral door-opener).

## 7. Herpesviridae (dsDNA) — the lifelong guests

*Kinship: large dsDNA, latency for life — the family that never leaves [D].*

### Herpes simplex virus 1 & 2

- **VIRUS:** dsDNA; ride: direct contact (oral/genital) [D].
- **DISEASE:** oral herpes; genital herpes; herpes encephalitis [D].
- **ILLNESS · SICKNESS:** the recurring sore-sickness — blisters that come back.
- **OLD NAMES:** "cold sores" / "fever blisters" — the folk names [D].
- **LABORATORY:** molecular — **PCR of lesion/CSF** (the gold standard) [D]; serology — **type-specific IgG** (tells past infection, and which type) [D]; culture — viral culture (slower, older) [D].
- **FUNGAL:** [GAP]. **BACTERIAL:** [GAP].
- **PESTILENCE:** ubiquitous, not pandemic — most of humanity carries one [D].
- **VACCINE (licensed):** none licensed [D] — stated plainly.
- **VACCINE (pipeline, separate):** candidates in trials; none past Phase 2 [D].

### Varicella-zoster virus

- **VIRUS:** dsDNA; ride: respiratory droplets + direct contact [D].
- **DISEASE:** chickenpox (first meeting); shingles (the return, decades later) [D].
- **ILLNESS · SICKNESS:** the itchy childhood sickness; the burning belt-sickness of age.
- **OLD NAMES:** **"chickenpox"** (debated origin — chickpea-sized, or a "chicken" mild smallpox) [D]; **"shingles"** — from Latin *cingulum*, belt; the same belt image as *zoster* (girdle), arrived through a different door [D].
- **LABORATORY:** molecular — PCR of lesion fluid [D]; serology — **IgG immunity testing** (are you protected?) [D].
- **FUNGAL / BACTERIAL:** [GAP].
- **PESTILENCE:** the universal childhood plague, pre-vaccine [D].
- **VACCINE (licensed):** **Varivax** — live-attenuated, chickenpox [D]; **Shingrix** — recombinant subunit (glycoprotein E + adjuvant), shingles — the modern subunit success [D].

### Epstein-Barr virus

- **VIRUS:** dsDNA; ride: saliva — "the kissing disease" [D].
- **DISEASE:** infectious mononucleosis; Burkitt lymphoma (with malaria as co-factor); nasopharyngeal carcinoma [D].
- **ILLNESS · SICKNESS:** the exhausting sickness — weeks of fatigue.
- **OLD NAMES:** "glandular fever" (old British) [D].
- **LABORATORY:** serology — **heterophile antibody (Monospot)** [D]; **EBV panel: VCA-IgM (acute), VCA-IgG, EBNA (past)** — the staged serology that dates the infection [D]; molecular — PCR (viral load, immunocompromised) [D].
- **FUNGAL:** [GAP]. **BACTERIAL:** [GAP] — note: amoxicillin given during mono causes the famous rash; the drug-virus interaction, not a bacterial companion [D].
- **PESTILENCE:** not pandemic; the Burkitt–malaria co-factor story is the epidemic link [D].
- **VACCINE (licensed):** none licensed [D].
- **VACCINE (pipeline, separate):** mRNA-1189 / mRNA-1195 (Moderna, Phase 2) [D].

### Cytomegalovirus

- **VIRUS:** dsDNA; ride: bodily fluids, birth, transplant [D].
- **DISEASE:** CMV mononucleosis-like illness; congenital CMV; CMV disease in transplant recipients [D].
- **ILLNESS · SICKNESS:** silent in most; devastating in the newborn and the immunosuppressed.
- **OLD NAMES:** none in common use [D].
- **LABORATORY:** serology — **IgM/IgG + IgG avidity** (avidity dates the infection — low avidity means recent) [D]; molecular — **PCR viral load** (transplant monitoring) [D]; antigen — pp65 antigenemia (older method) [D].
- **FUNGAL:** **CMV ↔ *Aspergillus*** — CMV infection raises invasive aspergillosis risk in transplant recipients; the virus opens the fungal door [D]. *(Cross-link: FT L2 companions.)*
- **BACTERIAL:** [GAP].
