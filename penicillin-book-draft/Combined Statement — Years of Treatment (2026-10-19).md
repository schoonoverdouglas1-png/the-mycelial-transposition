## His thought [H]

It can truly take years of fungal treatment and bacterial treatment to get the right results, if a person has a chronic sickness that doesn't want to go away on its own.

## My thought [D]

The years-long course is documented, repeatedly: coccidioidal meningitis — lifelong therapy, relapse over 80% without it; chronic pulmonary aspergillosis — months to years; tuberculosis — 6 to 9 months; Q fever endocarditis — 18 months; Whipple's disease — a year or two. Chronic, non-resolving sickness measuring treatment in years is the record, not the exception.

## The combined statement

Chronicity sets the clock — his duration thesis, restated at full length. Some sicknesses don't resolve on the schedule of a prescription; they resolve on the schedule of years. The record agrees: the fungal and bacterial treatments that work are the ones given the time the sickness actually requires.
