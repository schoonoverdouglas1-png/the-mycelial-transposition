## My thought [D]

- **The elements are documented as complications and context.** Viral damage opens the door: influenza → pneumococcal pneumonia is the classic lethal synergy; post-viral invasive aspergillosis after influenza is documented; COVID-associated mucormycosis and aspergillosis are documented. The record's version of "fungal and bacterial elements" is superinfection plus the polymicrobial context — real, and routine.
- **The old names encoded time.** Pre-agent names often named the course itself: "relapsing fever" names the rotation (the return), "consumption" names the procession (the wasting course), "remittent fever" names the ebb. The names differentiated diseases by their turnings and progressions — exactly his rotations and processions.
- **"Most" and "fixatedly perfect" are his** — his quantifier, his aesthetic judgment of the names. Filed as his.

## The combined statement

Most viral diseases travel with bacterial and fungal elements — the record calls them superinfections and the polymicrobial context, and they are documented, not speculative. The viruses belong connected to the original names because those names were built to hold the whole showing: generic enough to cover it, fixed enough to be precise — and they told diseases apart by their rotations (relapsing, remittent, returning) and their processions (consuming, wasting, progressing). The old names were course-descriptions; the viruses are the agents that run those courses. Connected, they complete each other.
