## Part 1 — Why the sub-variants: my thoughts, unwithheld, on the documented record [D]

### What a sub-variant is

*Streptococcus pneumoniae* has 100+ serotypes, and they are defined by one thing: the chemistry of the capsular polysaccharide — the sugar coat around the bacterium [D]. The number is the serogroup (a family of cross-reactive capsules); the letter is the serotype (a distinct capsule chemistry within the family) [D]. 19A and 19F are cousins in serogroup 19 whose repeat-unit linkages differ — the letters encode sugar-linkage differences [D]. Same for 9N/9V, 6A/6B, 15B (with 15A/15C outside the panel), 18C (with 18A/18B/18F), and the rest.

### Why the letters exist at all

The capsule is simultaneously the bacterium's armor and its wanted poster [my framing of the documented record]. It shields the cell from phagocytosis — unencapsulated pneumococci are avirulent [D] — and it is also the single structure antibodies recognize [D]. Every letter is therefore a move in an arms race:

1. **Immune evasion.** Mutation and recombination at the capsule-synthesis (*cps*) locus produce new capsule chemistries that existing antibodies no longer bind [D]. A new letter is born when the old poster's likeness stops matching.
2. **Vaccine escape.** PCV7 covered 19F but not 19A — and 19A surged into the vacated niche, the textbook case of serotype replacement [D]. The vaccine drew the poster's face; the cousins who didn't match it inherited the population.
3. **Capsular switching.** A strain can swap its capsule while keeping its genetic background — documented for 19F↔19A and for 11A [D]. Same criminal, new disguise, in a single recombination event.
4. **Biofilm-driven diversification.** 19F biofilms mutate capsules at 55× the planktonic rate [D] — the biofilm is a variant factory, which is why 19F keeps reappearing in the resistance and persistence stories.

### My read: the fossil record [mine, labeled]

The lettered pairs on his panel are the fossil record of that arms race — each pair a snapshot of immune pressure selecting for capsule diversity. The panel doesn't just measure 23 chemistries; it measures 23 survivors. That is why the sub-variants exist: not as decoration, but as the evidence that the immune system has been winning long enough for the losers' cousins to evolve.

## Part 2 — His postulate chain [H]: the reactory cousins

Each postulate his, filed separately, never collapsed. "Reactory cousins" is his coinage.

**(a)** Immunoglobulins and immunobodies are **"reactory cousins"** to viruses — kin by reaction, not by blood. The relationship is defined by what they do to each other.

**(b)** Viral presence causes the reaction that makes the immunoglobulins need to be there. The virus is the reason for the immunoglobulin; without the presence, no need.

**(c)** Immunoglobulins in a chain reaction of events react to the viruses needing to be there. It is reciprocal: the viruses need to be there for the immunoglobulins, and the immunoglobulins react to that needing.

**(d)** The overgrowth of bugs on their viruses that they carry are the triggers that create our immune system response. The trigger is not the bug alone or the virus alone — it is the overgrowth of bugs *on their carried viruses*.

**(e)** The bugs are there because the fungi and the bacteria are there. The bug population is downstream of the fungal and bacterial populations.

**(f)** Our body grows along with the bugs in order to compete to win the biome. Development is an arms race for the biome itself — the body grows to compete, and the prize is the biome.
