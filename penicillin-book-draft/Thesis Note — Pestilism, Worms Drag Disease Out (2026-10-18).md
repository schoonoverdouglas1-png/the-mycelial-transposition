## His theses [H]

1. **Pestilism** (his coinage — the pest/parasite condition) needs to be looked upon as a good thing first and a bad thing last, because a pestilism in the body is part of the biome of life.

3. If we become too malnourished, then disease becomes invasive.

## Honest lines (do not drop)

- **Ringworm is not a worm [D]:** ringworm (tinea/dermatophytosis) is a FUNGAL skin infection — the name is a misnomer, exactly the kind his namology tracks. It does not belong in the worm list biologically, though the name put it there. Lungworms and hookworms are real helminths [D].
- **Thesis 1 [H→D]:** the biome framing has documented company — the hygiene/"old friends" hypothesis: helminths modulate immunity, and their absence is associated with increased autoimmune/inflammatory disease in some studies [D]. Parasites as biome members is a live scientific position, not just his.
- **Thesis 2 [H, with footholds]:** helminth larvae genuinely migrate through the lungs and can appear in sputum (hookworm, Ascaris, Strongyloides larval migration; Loeffler syndrome with cough) [D] — "worms in sputum when a person coughs" is real. Experimental helminth immunomodulation research exists [D]. That worms "eat the disease out of us" / "drag disease out" as a mechanism is his [H].
- **Thesis 3 [H→D]:** malnutrition impairing immunity and increasing invasive infection is well documented [D] — his "too malnourished, disease becomes invasive" parallels the established malnutrition–infection cycle.
