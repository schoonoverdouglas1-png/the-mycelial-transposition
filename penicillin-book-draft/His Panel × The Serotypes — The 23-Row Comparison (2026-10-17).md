## The two resistance machines — the documented mechanisms

His thesis ties methicillin resistance, penicillin, serotypes, and the fungal together. The record holds two separate, documented machines. They are laid side by side here — the joining is his.

**Machine 1: pneumococcal penicillin resistance** [D — PMC 2005; PNAS 2023; existing penicillin-resistant file]. Penicillin kills by binding penicillin-binding proteins (PBPs 1a, 2b, 2x) — the enzymes that build the cell wall. Resistant pneumococci carry PBPs with **reduced affinity**: the drug cannot grip its target. The altered *pbp* genes are **mosaics** — native pneumococcal DNA recombined with divergent blocks picked up from closely related streptococci (*S. mitis*, *S. oralis*), because *S. pneumoniae* is naturally transformable: it takes foreign DNA and splices it into its own chromosome. A functional *murM* gene (alternative cell-wall substrate) is required to express the resistance. Selection pressure does the rest: beta-lactam use kills the susceptibles, the altered survive, and the resistant clones spread globally as named international clones (Spain23F-1, Spain6B-2). His framing — **"borrowed armor"** — is filed as his; the mosaic mechanism is the documented thing it names.

**Machine 2: staphylococcal methicillin resistance** [D — ScienceDirect; Wikipedia/mecA]. The *mecA* gene, carried on the mobile element SCC*mec*, encodes PBP2a — an additional penicillin-binding protein with **decreased affinity for beta-lactams**. PBP2a and the native PBP2 work together so cell-wall synthesis continues with the drug present. Same target class (PBPs), different route (an added substitute protein, not a rewritten one). MRSA is *Staphylococcus aureus* carrying this gene — documented as a bacterium, no serotypes [D].

**The honest seam:** both machines work by changing the drug's grip on the wall-building enzymes — that is the documented kinship between penicillin resistance and methicillin resistance [D]. His step from there to "fungal based upon their methicillin resistance" is [H] — his to evidence. The record gives him the grip; the fungal is his.

### The honest box: MRSA/non-MRSA vs PSSP/PNSP — two frameworks, two organisms

Filed plainly, because the section asked for both [D]:

- **MSSA vs MRSA** is a *Staphylococcus aureus* framework. MSSA = methicillin-susceptible *S. aureus*. MRSA = methicillin-resistant *S. aureus*, carrying mecA on SCC*mec*, producing PBP2a. It is a staph framework — it does not apply to pneumococcal serotypes.
- **PSSP / PISP / PRSP (or PNSP)** is the *Streptococcus pneumoniae* framework. PSSP = penicillin-susceptible *S. pneumoniae*; PISP = intermediate; PRSP = resistant; PNSP = penicillin-nonsusceptible (intermediate + resistant). The mechanism is altered mosaic PBPs — the "borrowed armor" already documented in this file.
- The two are laid side by side here **without conflating them**. His expectation — *"that will tell me all things about what medicines work with each serotype and serotype sickness"* — is filed verbatim as his [H]; the honest line beside it: susceptibility patterns describe the organism in the lab, not the medicine for a person.
