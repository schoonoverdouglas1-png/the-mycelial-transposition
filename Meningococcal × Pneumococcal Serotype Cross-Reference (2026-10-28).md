## Part II — The meningitis bridge (the direct cross-reference)

Two different organisms, one showing. This is the cross-reference that matters, and it is a cross-reference by **showing and capsule logic**, not by identity — no meningococcal serogroup maps to any pneumococcal serotype, and nothing below claims otherwise.

- **Meningococcal side:** serogroups A, B, C, W, Y — five letter codes, one organism, capsule chemistry deciding vaccine coverage, with B as the exception that forces protein-based vaccination [D + H→D].
- **Pneumococcal side:** meningitis group, ten serotypes — 1, 3, 4, 7F, 12F, 14, 15B, 17F, 18C, 23F — ten capsule chemistries in one organism, grouped by the same showing [D — §226].
- **Shared:** capsule chemistry predicts vaccine coverage in both systems [D]. That is why the lettering is not arbitrary in either system — unlike the C-clustering in his Architecture essay, where different Greek roots share only the letter C in English transliteration (a spelling artifact, not a family) [H + D roots].
- **Different:** code shape (letters vs number-letters); organism count (one vs one, but twenty-three chemistries vs five); vaccine technology (conjugate for A/C/W/Y, protein for B; conjugate + polysaccharide across the pneumococcal lists).
- **The seam (his thesis, held):** some letter systems track biochemistry and some track spelling. Meningococcal lettering and pneumococcal number-lettering both track biochemistry. Hold both truths at once — that is the Architecture essay's point, applied here instead of argued in the abstract.
